## 1. Result in brief

- **Where the list is.** The DRC's national list is a set of tables annexed to a Ministry of Health order on narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances:
  - Arrêté ministériel n°1250/CAB/MIN/S/0002/DC/OWE/2021 of 27 February 2021, *fixant les conditions d'exploitation des stupéfiants et des substances psychotropes à des fins médicales et scientifiques* (setting the conditions for the use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances for medical and scientific purposes);
  - as modified and completed, and re-issued in full (123 articles), by Arrêté ministériel n°1250/CAB/MIN/SPHP/038/DCA/CPh/2022 of 24 November 2022.
  - Art. 83 of the 2022 text: the substances 'sont classés dans les tableaux I, II et III, en annexe' (are classed in Tables I, II and III, annexed).
- **The annex cannot be read online.**
  - The two copies of the 2022 text and the one copy of the 2021 text all end at the signature. None of the three is on a government site.
  - The regulator's own copy of the 2021 order no longer loads.
  - The regulator's current site shows 'Pas de publication' in its legal library.
  - The Journal officiel's own site sells issues and gives no free text.
- **So no rows were recorded and nothing was guessed**, as instructed for this country. All 77 items are unread. None is put in `not_found`, because absence from a list that was not read cannot be shown.
- **The editor must decide** (section 7):

  - whether the generic clause in art. 84 should give "covered by reference" rows;
  - how to treat cannabis.

## 2. What could be read

### The 2022 order (the current text)

- **Identity.** 'Fait à Kinshasa, le 24 novembre 2022'. It is signed by the Minister of Public Health, Hygiene and Prevention, Mbungani Mbanda Jean-Jacques. It enters into force on signature (art. 123).
- **Publication.** Leganet's table of contents places it in the Journal officiel, numéro spécial, 6 March 2023, from col. 35. The next text starts at col. 87.
- **Preamble.**
  - It cites the 1961 Single Convention (as amended by the 1972 Protocol), the 1971 Convention, the 1933 pharmacy ordinance (Ordonnance n°27bis/Hyg. du 15 mars 1933) and Law n°18/035 of 13 December 2018 on public health, among other texts.
  - It "Revu" (reviews) the 2021 order.
  - It cites no national narcotics law (two readings).
- **Definitions (art. 3):**
  - 'Stupéfiants': natural or synthetic substances 'utilisées comme analgésique majeur' (used as major analgesics).
  - 'Substances psychotropes': natural or synthetic substances 'agissant sur le psychisme' (acting on the psyche).
  - 'Cannabis' is the flowering or fruiting tops. 'Plante de cannabis' is any plant of the genus. 'Résine de cannabis' is the separated resin.
  - 'Tableaux I, II, III et IV': these 'contiennent les listes des stupéfiants, ou des préparations annexées au présent Arrêté' (contain the lists of narcotic drugs, or of the preparations, annexed to this Order). They may be 'modifiées de temps à autre conformément à l'article 3 de la Convention unique de 1961'.
- **Classification (Chapter VI, arts. 83 to 87):**
  - **Art. 83:** the controlled substances and their preparations are classed in Tables I, II and III, annexed, according to the control measures that apply.
  - **Art. 84** opens with a generic clause. All plants and substances 'classées comme stupéfiants ou psychotropes par les Conventions internationales' go into the tables, as do those classified under the conventions, their preparations, and other plants and substances dangerous to public health. These 'sont inscrites à l'un des trois tableaux suivants', by the risk their abuse poses and by medical value:
    - Tableau I: 'plantes et substances à haut risque dépourvues d'intérêt en médecine' (high risk, no medical value);
    - Tableau II: high risk, of medical value;
    - Tableau III: 'à risque' (at risk), of medical value.
  - **Art. 85:** plants and substances are entered under their international non-proprietary name or, failing that, another name.
  - **Art. 86:** preparations are subject to the regime of the substances they contain.
  - **Art. 87:**
    - It allows exemptions for some preparations of Table II and III substances.
    - 'Les préparations contenant une substance inscrite au tableau I sont prohibées' (preparations containing a Table I substance are prohibited).
    - 'L'Acorep peut modifier les tableaux' (ACOREP may modify the tables), by new entry, removal or transfer.
  - Art. 3 speaks of four tables, but arts. 83 and 84 set up three. The text does not explain a fourth.
- **Cannabis.** Art. 100, in the chapter on cannabis for medical and scientific use, allows medical cultivation of varieties with THC 'inférieures ou égales à 0,2%' and high in CBD.
- **Other named substances.** The body names few substances. Reading 1 found only opium preparations (art. 77) and THC and CBD (art. 100). This was not re-read, and nothing depends on it.
- **No annex.** Both copies end at the signing line.

- **Number.** The transmittal letter prints 1250/CAB/MIN/S/0002/DC/OWE/2021. The heading's OCR is garbled.

  - 'du 27 février 2021' in the second transmittal block;
  - 21 février 2021 in the heading and signing line, as OCR'd.
  - The 2022 order cites it as 'du 27 février 2021'.
- **Contents.** 101 articles. It refers to Tableaux I to IV but prints no annex. Its preamble cites the 1961 Single Convention and no national narcotics law (two readings).

### The 1903 ordinance-law on hemp

- **Title.** 'Ordonnance-loi du 22 janvier 1903 approuvée par décret du 1er mars 1903. – Mesures contre l'usage de fumer le chanvre' (measures against the smoking of hemp).
- **Art. 1.** It bans 'La culture, la vente, le transport et la détention du chanvre à fumer' (the cultivation, sale, transport and possession of smoking hemp), and its use, smoked or consumed in any other way.
- **Form.** 7 articles, with no list, no publication source and no amendment note. Whether it is still in force was not established.
- **Where filed.** It is the only text filed under '325 Stupéfiants' in droitcongolais.info's penal-law index.

## 5. Method of reading

- **Readings.**
  - Each decisive passage was read twice, in WebFetch calls with differently worded prompts and output formats ("ARTICLE | words" lines against "words ;; article" lines, summaries against fragments).
  - Long passages (arts. 3 and 84) were read a third time in short fragments, in both copies.
  - Where two readings differed, a third reading was taken.
- **Comparison by script.**
  - Python ran inline from the shell, with the readings pasted in.
  - The script normalised whitespace, apostrophes, spacing before colons, final stops and the OCR forms "Il" (for II) and "1,11 et 111" (for I, II et III). One row was compared without accents.
  - 44 rows were compared: 41 agree, 3 differ, and all 3 are settled (section 6).
  - The Leganet and droitcongolais.info copies of the 2022 order agree on arts. 3 ('Tableaux'), 83, 84, 87 and 100 and on the signing line.
- **Summariser limits.**
  - WebFetch quotes at most about 125 characters at a time, so long passages were read in fragments.
  - The 2021 scan's OCR garbles numbers and dates (for example 'SCTENTIFIQUES', '27 ffutrier 2021').
- **No matching was done**, because no list entry could be read.

## 8. Notes for the editor

**If the annex is obtained, read it under the order's own rules:**
- Art. 85 lists substances by INN or, failing that, another name.
- Art. 86 puts preparations under the regime of their contents.
- Art. 87 prohibits preparations of Table I substances.
- The art. 84 clause should be quoted wherever the annex is silent on a UN-scheduled item.

**`medical_use`:** the table headings in art. 84 state each table's medical value ('dépourvues d'intérêt en médecine' for Table I; 'présentant un intérêt en médecine' for Tables II and III). This could fill `medical_use` once an item's table is known.
