## 1. The Bangladeshi list

The list is the **First Schedule (প্রথম তপশিল)** of the **Narcotics Control Act, 2018** (মাদকদ্রব্য নিয়ন্ত্রণ আইন, ২০১৮; Act No. 63 of 2018, 14 Nov 2018).

- **Definition.** s. 2(29): 'narcotics' (মাদকদ্রব্য) means (a) "any object mentioned in the First Schedule", or (b) a mixture or solution that contains a narcotic.
- **Classes.** s. 2(9): the Class-A, B and C narcotics are those so marked in the Schedule. Each class has numbered serials (ক্রমিক). Within a serial, the substances form an unnumbered list separated by commas, so no item number is printed.
  - **Class A (‘ক’ শ্রেণি), serials 1-8:**
    1. opium poppy;
    2. coca plant, coca leaves, all cocaine derivatives;
    3. opium;
    4. 49 named opioids and others (e.g. fentanyl, heroin, ketamine, mitragynine);
    5. amphetamine-type substances;
    6. 114 names (opioids, cannabis resin, cathinone, mescaline, psilocine, THC);
    7. generic clause;
    8. precursor chemicals.
  - **Class B (‘খ’ শ্রেণি), serials 1-5:**
    1. cannabis plants and products;
    2. any other intoxicating plant;
    3. ethyl alcohol;
    4. 76 lines (hallucinogens, stimulants, barbiturates, GHB, PCP, salvinorin A, "Synthetic Canabinoids", tramadol, caffeine, shisha);
    5. generic clause.
  - **Class C (‘গ’ শ্রেণি), serials 1-4:**
    1. tari, panchai;
    2. methyl alcohol;
    3. 40 names, mostly benzodiazepines, plus zolpidem;
    4. generic clause.
- **Explanation.** It closes the Schedule and defines "Psycotropic/Psycoactive Substance" (A4-6, B4-5, C3-4), "Salt" and "Shisha".
- **Second Schedule.** It is the narcotics duty (মাদকশুল্ক), so it does not list substances and is not used.
- **Amendment.** s. 65: the Government may amend any Schedule by notification in the official Gazette.
- **Authorities.** The Department of Narcotics Control is under the Security Services Division of the Ministry of Home Affairs.

## 4. Method of reading

- **Two readings.** Every chunk was read twice through WebFetch, with differently worded prompts and output formats ("A4 | name" against "4 ;; name").
  - English text: Class A (from offset 84000); Classes B and C with the Explanation (from offset 89000); s. 2 clauses 9, 13, 19, 29 and s. 65.
  - Gazette: the Schedule from offset 48000 to the end.
  - bdlaws: the s. 36(1) table (18 rows) and the s. 2 footnotes.
  - Act No. 26 of 2020: a section-by-section reading, then a search for তপশিল/তফসিল.
- **Script comparison.** The two readings were compared by Python scripts, with both readings pasted verbatim into each script. The Gazette's 263 Latin names were then compared by script with the English text, serial by serial. No reading files were saved, since only the two output files may be written.
- **Matching.** The 77 items were matched by script against all 332 Schedule entries and descriptive lines, using name variants as search keys. Every hit was checked by eye.

## 6. Result

**Found: 45 of the 77 items.**
- 39 are named in the Schedule:
  - Class A serials 2, 4, 5 and 6: 22 items. The Bengali Gazette prints the same Latin names for 21 of them. For coca leaf, the Gazette shows only "Cocaine derivatives" at A2, and the Bengali s. 36(1) table on bdlaws names "কোকা পাতা" (coca leaf) at A2.
  - Class B serials 1 and 4: 12 items. The Gazette confirms 8 of the B4 names. Cannabis (B1) is in Bengali script only there; its Bengali name, "গাঁজা অথবা ভাং গাছ", comes from the s. 36(1) table.
  - Class C serial 3: 5 benzodiazepines.
- Five synthetic cannabinoids come under the class entry "Synthetic Canabinoids)" (sic) in B4. They are not named.
- Esketamine comes under the isomer wording of A7 (ketamine is A4). It is recorded as draft.

**Read only in the English text** (the Bengali was not read): phencyclidine, salvinorin A, tramadol, the five benzodiazepines and the five synthetic cannabinoids. They are ai-checked on the two English readings.

**Not found: 30 items, plus 2 "if named" items that are not named** (ayahuasca; peyote or San Pedro). All 30 are draft.

**Generic clauses recorded:**
- A7, B5 and C4: by-products and compounds, and "alkaloid, salt, isomer, analogue or agonists";
- B2: any intoxicating plant other than the Class A plants;
- s. 2(29)(b): mixtures;
- A2: "all cocaine derivatives" and any substance with more than 0.1% cocaine.

`medical_use` is null for every entry, because the Schedule states no medical use.
