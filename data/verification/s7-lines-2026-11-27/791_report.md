## May read as advice (the senior author decides)

- `cl_nic_nrt_forms`, `cl_nic_vapes_stopping`, `cl_nic_vapes_stopping_support`: trial findings ("did a little better",
  "about 4 more in 100"), but readers may take them as advice to combine patch and gum, or to switch to vaping.
- `cl_nic_smoking_health`: years gained by age at stopping are a finding, but they read as encouragement to quit.
- `cl_nic_cigars`: kept "risks rose with more cigars a day and with inhaling" as risk facts. I left out the figures for
  1 to 2 cigars a day (no clear rise), which could read as a safe amount.
- `cl_nic_pouches`: left out every milligram figure, nicotine peak times and how much nicotine pouches deliver compared
  with cigarettes. I kept one harm finding: the two strongest pouches raised blood pressure about as much as a cigarette.
- `cl_nic_nrt_harms`: left out "six trials in pregnancy did not detect more serious events": on its own it could read
  as "safe in pregnancy", and `cl_nic_smoke_or_nicotine` says nicotine harms the unborn baby.
