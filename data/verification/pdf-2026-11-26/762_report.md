## cl_amph_vs_mph: low, single study
Rests on Cortese 2018 (network meta-analysis of 133 trials: symptoms, stopping for side effects), Farhat 2025 (102 trials: blood pressure,
pulse; it reuses Cortese's searches and shares three authors, so not independent) and Efron 1997 (one blinded crossover, 125 children, no
placebo). Starts high; risk of bias −1 (most trials of unclear risk; Cortese prints no funnel-plot result; 85% of Farhat's trials industry
funded; Efron some concerns); indirectness −1 (the comparison runs mostly through placebo, in adults only; "amphetamines" pools products;
weeks only). Imprecision is not taken down because the statement says more stopping on amphetamines (OR 1.60, 0.94–2.73) is neither shown
nor ruled out, rather than "did not differ"; that comparison and the adult ones alone would be very low. Relation "is associated with": an
indirect comparison is not randomized. The abstract's "head-to-head" is the claim's one quotation, with its correction. Section 12 (the
medicines as treatment); the editor may prefer 5.
Missing: Cortese's appendix (trial list, so whether Efron is one of the 133; GRADE reasons; funnel plots) and Comment
(10.1016/S2215-0366(18)30295-5); Farhat's appendix (licensed-dose sensitivity numbers, outliers, 24-week data) and Comment (p. 319, no DOI
printed); any long head-to-head trial.

## cl_amph_vs_mph_psychosis: low, single study
Moran 2019 alone (claims cohort; ROBINS-I moderate): starts low; nothing taken down for the association as worded (matched starters,
consistent unadjusted, adjusted and matched estimates, near-null negative controls); no upgrade (HR 1.65, below 2). Absolute first: 343 of
221,846 over a median of 113 to 162 days (1 in 647, editors' calculation; the paper's "1 in 660" kept beside it); 1.78 against 2.83 per
1,000 person-years; difference 1.05 (editors'). "About twice" rests on crude shares inflated by longer follow-up after amphetamine (about
83,700 against 59,600 person-years from the printed counts and rates, editors'); rate ratio 1.59, HR 1.65.
Missing: the Supplementary Appendix (results by database, the 365-day analysis by starting drug); any replication (none on file; the two
reviews did not study psychosis).
