## cl_amph_vs_mph: low, single study
Rests on Cortese 2018 (network meta-analysis, 133 trials: symptoms, stopping for side effects), Farhat 2025 (102 trials: blood pressure,
pulse; reuses Cortese's searches, three shared authors: not independent) and Efron 1997 (one blinded crossover, 125 children, no placebo).
Starts high; risk of bias −1 (trials mostly of unclear risk; no funnel-plot result in Cortese; 85% of Farhat's trials industry funded);
indirectness −1 (comparison mostly through placebo, in adults only; "amphetamines" pools products; weeks only). Imprecision not taken down
because the statement says more stopping on amphetamines (OR 1.60, 0.94–2.73) is neither shown nor ruled out, not "did not differ"; alone,
that and the adult comparisons would be very low. "Is associated with": an indirect comparison is not randomized. The abstract's
"head-to-head" is the one quotation, corrected. Section 12; the editor may prefer 5.
Missing: Cortese's appendix (trial list: is Efron among the 133?; GRADE reasons; funnel plots) and Comment
(10.1016/S2215-0366(18)30295-5); Farhat's appendix (licensed-dose numbers, outliers, 24-week data) and Comment (p. 319, no DOI printed).

## cl_amph_vs_mph_psychosis: low, single study
Moran 2019 alone (claims cohort, ROBINS-I moderate): starts low; nothing taken down for the association as worded (matched new starters,
consistent unadjusted, adjusted and matched estimates, near-null negative controls); no upgrade (HR 1.65). Absolute first: 343 of 221,846
over a median of 113 to 162 days (1 in 647, editors'; the paper's 1 in 660 kept beside it); 1.78 against 2.83 per 1,000 person-years;
difference 1.05 (editors'). "About twice" rests on crude shares inflated by longer follow-up after amphetamine (about 83,700 against 59,600
person-years from the printed counts and rates, editors'); rate ratio 1.59.
Missing: the Supplementary Appendix (results by database; 365-day analysis by starting drug); any replication (none on file).
