## cl_lsd_nbome: very low, single study, draft (narrative review only)

Rests on Wood et al. (2015), read in full; every page used checked on renderings. Descriptive: low, −1 risk of bias
(published cases selected for severity; drug found in the person's own samples in 15 of 29, editors' count; other drugs),
−1 indirectness (second-hand, as the statement says).

Printed figures kept, each discrepancy stated in the claim: seizures 37.9% (11 of 29) though the case descriptions give 12;
kidney injury 5 printed, 6 described; the abstract's "detected in eight fatalities" against laboratory detection in five.
Texas seizures 4% = 1 of 25 (editors' calculation). Believed LSD: the Burning Man woman ('acid' blotter, mainly 25C-NBOMe),
three Belgians ('synthetic LSD'), and three deaths (Houston, Belgium, Richmond).

Missing: a drug-checking study of blotters sold as LSD (only that could say how often they hold an NBOMe); the primary
reports (Hill 2013, Forrester 2014, Walterscheid 2014, Poklis 2014; Armenian & Gerona 2014, 10.1016/j.ajem.2014.04.047, the
only DOI printed); the 2014 EMCDDA risk assessment. Card sc_wood_2015 and the reference record agree with the PDF.

## cl_benzo_designer: very low, replicated, "insufficient evidence"

Rests on Carpenter et al. (2019; online-first PDF, PDF pages) and Brunetti et al. (2021), both read in full. No source
compares designer with prescribed benzodiazepines or counts the people who used them, hence the relation. Descriptions:
low, −1 risk of bias, −1 indirectness. Replicated: two independent sources agree (mostly sedation; deaths without other
drugs rare).

Recount: parsing Table 1 by its ruled rows gives 254 rows matching every printed total (141 deaths, 63 DUID, 44 ED, 3, 2,
1). Death entries with an opioid 115 of 141, loperamide or its metabolite 2, no other substance 3: identical to reader 736.
Four double entries confirmed on the rendered pages (pp. 10/22, 11/26, 12/26, 13/27, all with an opioid): 111 of 137
counting each once; two identical entries on p. 31 may be a fifth. Forensic concentrations are left out of the claim.

Carpenter: Table 3 used (coma 4%, agitation 9%), not the text (8%, 14%); the 330% rise spans the 2016 coding change
(etizolam alone 26 to 60); the impossible per-million rates and the Discussion's 23% and 3% not used; 234 + 234 is not 474.

Card against PDF: sc_brunetti_2021's polysubstance field lists oxycodone among death-entry opioids; in Table 1 oxycodone
appears only in impaired-driving rows (pp. 17, 25). Otherwise both cards agree. Reference records: brunetti_2021 drops the
middle names (Marilyn A. Huestis, Francesco Paolo Busardò); carpenter_2019 lowercases "National Poison Data System".

Missing (no DOIs printed): Papsun et al. 2021, Kriikku et al. 2020, Shearer et al. 2015, the 2016 NPDS annual report,
Carpenter's supplement. No file compares designer benzodiazepines with prescribed ones, or with and without opioids.
