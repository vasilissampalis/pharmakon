## Writer 754: cl_dc_pill_logo (pdf1126_w754.json; 754_simple.json; 754_report.md; 754_calculations.py)
- Very low, single study (descriptive; −1 risk of bias, −1 indirectness); 14 anchors ai-located. No study measured how much tablets with the same logo differ. Against lab results for matching pills, 39.7 in 100 online reports stated over 10 mg too much MDMA and 17.6 in 100 over 10 mg too little; the authors' 15.3 in 100 "dangerous" does not count stating too much. - For the editor: section 11 (with the other drug-checking answers) or section 2; whether cl_mdma_pill_content's flag should point to this answer.
- Cards against the PDFs: Vrolijk card says "most" reports named no source (PDF: "often"); Vogels card counts weight among the features that must match (PDF: measured, not a matching criterion). Cole et al. (2002) not on file (APA reference in the report).
