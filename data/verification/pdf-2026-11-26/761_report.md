# Reader 761: tramadol (items 17–18), 26 November 2026

Ryan & Isbister (2015) and Senay et al. (2003) read in full; Rostam-Abadi et al. (2020) pp. 1–3, 7, 13–25 and 27. Every table and figure used checked on a rendered page (forest plots at 300 dpi); intervals, pool totals and Senay's shares recomputed.

## Claims

**17. `cl_opi_tramadol_overdose`** (new, section 6): 71 Australian admissions after more than 400 mg (51 with other medicines) and the Iranian review's pools. Seizures 8/71 (11 in 100; 5–21), as often after tramadol alone (2/20) as with other medicines (6/51); coma and respiratory depression (13/71) not split for tramadol alone. Iran: seizures 34.6 in 100 (prediction interval 12.5–60.9), deaths 0.7 in 100 (0–10.0). Serotonin toxicity 0/71 rules out only rates above 5.1 in 100 in such overdoses. **Very low** (low −1 risk of bias, −1 inconsistency); replicated. Naloxone response and seizure timing appear as hospital findings only.

**18. `cl_opi_tramadol_withdrawal`** (new, section 8): Senay's 422 of 1,248 reports rated withdrawal (33 in 100 by Table 3, not the abstract's nearly 40), 55 atypical (1 in 8); reported cases only (capture about 10 in 100, the FDA estimate the authors cite); manufacturer-funded; no comparison with other opioids. Plus two Iranian case reports. **Very low** (low −2 risk of bias); single study. The paper's management advice (about 75 consultations, no counts) is left out.

## Missing papers that would change these

- Seizures at prescribed doses: Gasse et al. (2000), Pharmacotherapy 20, 629–634; Gardner et al. (2000), Pharmacotherapy 20, 1423–1431 (Ryan's references; no DOI printed).
- Serotonin toxicity with antidepressants: Nelson & Philbrick (2012), Ann Pharmacother 46, 1712–1716.
- Whether atypical withdrawal is particular to tramadol: Fishbain et al. (1988), J Clin Psychopharmacol 8, 441–442 (a letter in Senay's references on atypical withdrawal after oxycodone detoxification); Cicero et al. (1999), Drug Alcohol Depend 57, 7–22.
- Rostam-Abadi's supplements Data S1–S5.
