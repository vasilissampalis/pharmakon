## 1. The Pakistani lists

The national law is the **Control of Narcotic Substances Act, 1997** (CNSA, Act No. XXV of 1997). It is enforced by the Ministry of Narcotics Control with the Anti-Narcotics Force (ANF), and DRAP allocates quotas for controlled medicines (DRAP page, read once). The Act has three kinds of list:

- **Narcotic drugs.** These are defined in s. 2 itself (two readings, identical):
  - (d) "cannabis (hemp)": the resin (charas), including hashish oil; the flowering or fruiting tops (bhang, siddhi, ganja); mixtures and drinks. Seed and leaves are excluded when not accompanied by the tops.
  - (e) cannabis plant: any plant of the genus cannabis.
  - (g) coca derivative: crude cocaine; ecgonine; cocaine and its salts; preparations above 0.1% cocaine.
  - (h) coca leaf.
  - (u) opium derivative: medicinal opium; prepared opium; morphine and its salts; diacetylmorphine ("diamorphine or heroin") and its salts; preparations above 0.2% morphine. Codeine and thebaine are not named.
  - (q) manufactured drug: "all coca derivatives, medicinal hemp, opium derivatives, cannabis in any form", stalk and top mixtures, acetic anhydride, and (q)(ii) "any other narcotic substance" the Federal Government declares by Gazette notification.
  - (s) narcotic drug: "coca leaf, cannabis, heroin, opium, poppy straw and all manufactured drugs".
- **Psychotropic substances.** Under s. 2(za), these are the substances in **Schedule-I**, plus any the Federal Government declares by notification.
  - The Schedule is headed "[See Section 2(za)] PSYCHOTROPIC SUBSTANCES" and has three columns: international non-proprietary name; other non-proprietary or trivial names; chemical name.
  - Rows with an INN carry serial numbers. Substances printed without an INN carry no number in any copy read: DET, DMA, DMHP, DMT, DOET, MDMA, mescaline, 4-methylaminorex, MMDA, N-ethyl MDA, N-hydroxy MDA, parahexyl, PMA, psilocine, levomethamphetamine and butobarbital.
- **Controlled substances (precursors).** Under s. 2(k), these are listed in **Schedule-II**, added by Act XX of 2022. Its Divisions I and II are Tables I and II of the 1988 Convention.

**Related federal law.** The Cannabis Control and Regulatory Authority Act, 2024 (assent 24 Sep 2024; read twice) is a licensing law for cannabis and cannabinoids:
- It names "cannabidiol (CBD)" and THC as cannabinoids in s. 2(1)(c).
- It applies the CNSA's trial provisions.
- It does not amend the CNSA or its Schedules.

Its 2026 amendment is image-only on the National Assembly site. The bill as passed by the National Assembly, read once, changes only the proviso to s. 7(4).

**Provinces.** The Sindh Control of Narcotic Substances Act, 2024 (Sindh Act VIII of 2024) extends to the whole of Sindh (s. 1(2)); it was read once, for title and extent. Search results also show official copies of a Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Act (2019, amended 2025), a Balochistan Act (2026) and a Punjab Bill (2025). None of these was read for its lists.

## 4. Method of reading

- **Two readings.** Every list was read twice through WebFetch, with differently worded prompts and output formats. For example, Schedule-I was read as "N | INN | OTHER | CHEM" and then as "serial ;; first-column name ;; second-column name ;; chemical description".
- **Tool limits.** The summarizing model caps verbatim quotes at about 125 characters, and sometimes refused long copies:

  - For Urdu-text segments A (start to FLUDIAZEPAM) and C (PHENCYCLIDINE to the end), full-line second readings were refused, so the second readings list names only.

  - Segment B (FLUDIAZEPAM to PHENCYCLIDINE) was read twice in full.
- **Script comparison.** Python scripts compared the readings, with both readings pasted verbatim into each script as inline input. No reading files were saved, because only the two output files were to be written.
  - Schedule rows: compared on serial number plus normalised name; chemical names compared by prefix; column splits tested on the joined text.
  - Urdu text: names tested in order (names-only reading against full-line reading); segment B compared line by line and as joined text.
  - Definitions: compared clause by clause after removing footnote markers.
- **Matching.** The 77 items were matched by script against all rows read (Schedule-I, s. 2 and the legible Schedule-II entries), using name variants, INNs, abbreviations and chemical-name fragments as keys, with a fuzzy check for misprints. Every hit was then checked by eye.
