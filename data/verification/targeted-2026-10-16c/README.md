# Targeted check, round c (2026-10-16)

51 anchors and 4 appraisals, checked at the stated place by an independent reader (AI reader 3) who had not located them;
papers read in the app's browser (PubMed Central and publishers' pages). Files: `tasks.json` and `appraisal_tasks.json`
(what the reader was given), `out.json` and `appraisals.json` (the reader's results), `out_editor.json` (what was applied),
`patch.json` (the anchor patch), `text_patch.json` (claim wording aligned with the corrections).

Results: 30 confirmed, 13 corrected, 5 wrong location (text right, place corrected), 3 not checked.

Editor's decisions:
- Applied as returned, except: n5 and n20 reworded person-first (content unchanged); n28–30 removed, because the
  source (McKetin et al., 2013, JAMA Psychiatry) could not be opened (bot check), so no check took place. Those three
  anchors stay as they were and need the PDF.
- Claim wording changed to match the corrected anchors: dopamine damage (VMAT2 probe binding, read by its authors as low
  stored dopamine; the Parkinson's count includes same-date diagnoses), Parkinson's disease (codes on or after the first
  stimulant code; follow-up 6.8 and 7.1 years), mortality (imputed ratios standardized for age, sex, location and
  calendar years; the drug-type caveat as the reviewers put it), psychosis course (stress-triggered relapses as
  Japanese researchers' description, hedged), withdrawal (use within 7 days is a description, not an entry rule), grey
  matter (one study's abstinence is at the mood assessment, not at scanning), naltrexone–bupropion (two or more missing
  samples, not any, counted as non-response).
- Appraisals: Kevil 2019, Bramness 2012 and Kish 2008 moved from AMSTAR 2 to the editors' narrative-review checklist
  (METHODS §5.3), overall high, with the reader's domain judgments; a second reading of the checklist is still needed.
  Stockings 2019: AMSTAR 2 items 5, 6, 11 and 13 set to "yes" (AMSTAR 2 offers no partial rating for them); the overall
  rating (low) was not changed, since raising it is a change of judgment for the senior author.
- Reporting errors the reader noticed in the papers (not errors of ours; for the senior author's information): Curtin
  2015 gives two different confidence intervals for one estimate (Abstract vs Results); Stockings 2019 gives two
  intervals for the cardiovascular estimate (Abstract vs Table 2); Han's "2015 to 2019" changes are calculated from the
  2016 values; the Trivedi registry's stage 2 wording repeats stage 1.
