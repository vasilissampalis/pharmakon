# Reader 685: tolerance, a new place, stopping opioids (items 14–17), 14 November 2026

All five PDFs read in full (Siegel 1984, a scan, on rendered pages; every table and figure used checked on a rendering).

## Claims

**14. `cl_mix_opioid_tolerance_loss`** (patch 0.1.2 → 0.2.0; dry run passes). Strang 2003 added (anchors 6–9): 137 people followed a year after a 28-day London inpatient detox; all 3 overdose deaths, within 4 months, among the 37 who finished the whole programme, none among the 100 who left earlier; 4 of the 5 deaths (all causes) lived alone. **Very low**: risk of bias, indirectness (tolerance never measured), imprecision (3 deaths). No longer review-only; left at draft until round 2. `cl_gen_release_deaths` cross-reference updated (wording). Missing: Bird & Hutchinson (2003, Addiction 98, 185–190); Merrall et al. (2010, doi 10.1111/j.1360-0443.2010.02990.x).

**15. `cl_opi_conditioned_tolerance`**. Siegel 1984 (10 survivors recalling the overdose ~5 years later, no comparison; 7 unusual, only 3 a new place or company) beside Siegel 2005 (the model; rat findings without figures). **Very low** (case series: −2, −1, −1). Missing: Siegel et al. (1982, doi 10.1126/science.7200260); Siegel et al. (2000, Exp Clin Psychopharmacol 8, 276–293).

**16. `cl_opi_treatment_mortality`**. Sordo 2017: methadone 11.3 in against 36.1 out per 1,000 person-years (16 cohorts, all one direction; largest register cohorts 5.8–6.9 against 12.4–17.6). The brief's check: **32.1 is the first four weeks after leaving against 13.5 for later time out of treatment** (5 cohorts), not against time in treatment (5.8). Start of methadone not significant; buprenorphine uncertain; no clear overdose rise after leaving (3 cohorts; in two, 18 of 79 early deaths coded overdose, editors' calculation). **Low** (no downgrade, no upgrade; rated as an association). Missing: the appendices (table B might separate planned tapers from dropping out); Kimber 2015 (doi 10.1016/S2215-0366(15)00366-1); Cousins 2016 (doi 10.1111/add.13087).

**17. `cl_opi_withdrawal_deaths`**. Darke 2017 only: 1 UK and 10 press-reported US deaths in custody; no rate; the rise asserted without counts. **Very low**, draft (editorial). Missing: Appendix S1. The 93% figure is attributed to Rich et al. (2015, Lancet 386, 350–359) but fits Larney et al. (2014, BMJ Open 4, e004666); left out.
