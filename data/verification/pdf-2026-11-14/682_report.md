## cl_mdma_empathy (new): low, replicated

Rests on Bedi 2010 (21 people; MDMA, methamphetamine, placebo), Hysek 2014 (32) and, from its card, Bedi 2009 (faces
unchanged). Starts high; risk of bias −1 (self-ratings, probable unblinding, selected subscales, many uncorrected
tests); imprecision −1 (20 to 32 people; empathy and money-game p = 0.019 to 0.047). At the top: loving and friendly
ratings after MDMA did not differ significantly from methamphetamine, only methamphetamine raised 'sociable', and fearful
faces were named correctly 0.55 against 0.63 (methamphetamine's 0.59 did not differ from MDMA's either). Basel:
emotional empathy about 5.0 against 4.4 of 9, positive scenes only; four negative emotions read worse; oxytocin rose
without tracking the changes. Fear alone would be moderate. Kirkpatrick 2014 unused: its closeness finding
is already in `cl_mdma_feelings`. Missing: Hysek, Domes & Liechti (2012, Psychopharmacology 222, 293–302), cited as
better recognition of positive emotions in the eyes test; Hysek's supplement (face means). No DOIs printed.

## cl_mdma_sex (new): very low, replicated

Zemishlany survey (35; every printed percentage matches Table I) and McElrath interviews (98; no counts). Descriptive:
starts low; risk of bias −1, indirectness −1 (no sampling frame; Israeli sample chosen for use before sex; 1990s).
Replicated: two independent samples, same kinds of experience, no frequencies. Missing: Buffum & Moser
(1986, J Psychoactive Drugs 18, 355–359); any controlled study; anything on MDMA with erection medicines.

## Patch cl_mdma_hyponatremia: very low, replicated (unchanged)

Henry 1998: 8 men, 40 mg base; vasopressin 1.14–1.88 to 2.46–9.16 pmol/L at 2 hours (one-tailed p = 0.006); sodium
down 1 to 3 in 7 of 8, all 140–142 (one-tailed p = 0.025); 3 later untreated occasions, never compared. **Henry measured
no urine, so it says nothing about difficulty peeing** (theme 29); that needs case reports of urinary retention, none on
file. Beyond the brief: one anchor from Hysek 2014 (no significant copeptin change, 32 people).
`cl_mdma_hyponatremia_risk` describes a copeptin study from the same Basel group second-hand (Simmler, Hysek & Liechti,
2011, J Clin Endocrinol Metab 96, 2844–2850); keep consistent. Baggott 2016 (10.1155/2016/2175896) still unread.
Existing myth-or-fact item carried to v 0.6.0.
