# Claim writer 683: questions 7–10 (round pdf-2026-11-14)

Four new drafts in `data/claims/_incoming/pdf1114_w683.json`; plain lines in `683_simple.json`; no patch. Numbers come from the PDFs; tables and figures were checked on rendered pages.

## cl_mdma_vs_mda: very low, single study
Baggott 2019 alone, rated observational (starts low): 12 MDA volunteers set beside other people's MDMA data from two earlier studies, recorded 6 h against 8. Risk of bias −1, imprecision −1. Heart rate rose less after MDA (20 against 30.6 bpm, p = 0.03), though the abstract says "similar"; "well-tolerated" has no adverse-event data; popularity is not answered. Would change it: Baggott 2016a (10.1177/0269881115626348) and 2016b (10.1155/2016/2175896), describing the MDMA volunteers; Baggott 2010 (10.1371/journal.pone.0014074), the same trial, perhaps stating how session order was set.

## cl_lsd_vs_psilocybin: low, single study
Holze 2022, randomized crossover, 28 people: starts high; risk of bias −1 (right drug named in at least 64–86% of sessions, editors' calculation; statistics mostly in the supplement), imprecision −1 ("alike in kind" rests on non-significant tests). Duration alone would be moderate. No dose equivalence given. The supplement (10.1038/s41386-022-01297-2) would settle the misprints. Brief against PDF: two means in Table 1 lie outside their ranges; the third misprint is LSD 100 µg's Tmax in the pharmacokinetics paragraph (p. 1183).

## cl_mix_mdma_alcohol: very low, single study
Hernández-López 2002, 9 men: starts high; risk of bias −1, imprecision −2 ("as drunk" and "as impaired at the worst point" are non-significant). Sedation fell, drunkenness did not (about 58 against 40 mm, read from Fig. 3, not significant). MDMA units misprinted (mg/dl; assay range 25–400 ng/ml). Brief against PDF: the unreported measures are "cardiovascular, endocrine, and other physiological"; temperature is not named, and the claim follows the paper. Left out for the editor: longer-lasting euphoria with both drugs (potentiation rule). Not read: Pacifici et al. 2001 (J Pharmacol Exp Ther 296, 207; no DOI printed); no source is cited for the cardiovascular data.

## cl_mix_mdma_lsd: low, single study
Straumann 2023, 24 people: starts high; risk of bias −1, imprecision −1; the blood-pressure and heart-rate rises alone would be moderate. Three nausea statements disagree. LSD lowered and delayed MDMA's peak (Table 2), which the text omits. Left out under the brief: the 1.5 h longer "any drug effect" (perhaps wanted as a risk fact) and the untested CYP2D6-antidepressant suggestion (a possible separate question). The supplement (10.1038/s41386-023-01609-0, Table S6) would settle nausea.
