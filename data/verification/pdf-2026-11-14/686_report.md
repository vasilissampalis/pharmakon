**18 `cl_can_strain_labels`: very low, single study, describes.** Smith et al. (2022): 89,923 US compliance samples; the
Indica/Hybrid/Sativa category comes from a website's database, matched by strain name, compared on terpenes only
(silhouette −0.03 against 0.27 and 0.38). Fig 8C recounted at 400 dpi: 26 names above chance, 13 not different, 2 below,
as the first reader found; the cluster χ² reproduces from percentages (22.28). Hazekamp & Fischedick (2012) only
qualifies. Low start, indirectness −1. Missing: Jikomes & Zoorob 2018 (doi:10.1038/s41598-018-22755-2), Vergara et al.
2020 (doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0236878), Elzinga et al. 2015 and Hazekamp et al. 2016 (no DOI printed), figures S1–S6,
and any study of effects.

**19 `cl_gen_family_craft`: moderate, replicated, increases.** Miller et al. (1999): 64, 30 and 13 in 100 entered
treatment within 6 months; Roozen et al. (2010): RR 3.25 (2.11–5.02), four US trials. High start, risk of bias −1
(unblinded, therapists nested, developer-supervised CRAFT, three of four trials from CRAFT's groups, review AMSTAR 2
critically low). Family wellbeing is worded as equal improvement without an untreated group; "almost three times" for
RR 2.15 is not adopted. **Editor to decide:** a separate publication-bias downgrade (small developer-run trials, no
registry search) would make it low; I counted that concern under risk of bias only. Missing: Sisson & Azrin 1986, Kirby
et al. 1999, Meyers et al. 2002 (no DOIs printed), Miller's drinking outcomes, trials after November 2008.

**20 `cl_benzo_stopping_suddenly`: very low, draft (narrative reviews only), insufficient evidence.** Lader (2011) and
Brett & Murnion (2015), second-hand: 15–30 in 100 after 4–6 weeks (Lader's own 1998 review); severe reactions mostly
anecdotal; the only seizure count is 2 of 11 prisoners at several times the usual dose (1961); Brett's seizure statements
are unreferenced, and Brett cites Lader. Brett's tapering schedule and dose thresholds and Lader's dose example and
equivalence table are deliberately left out. Missing: Hollister et al. 1961 (Psychopharmacologia 2, 63–68), Lader 1998
(CNS Drugs 10, 425–440), Ashton's guide, Petursson & Lader 1984 (no DOIs printed), any cohort counting seizures.

**21 `cl_gen_festival_policing`: very low, single study, insufficient evidence.** Hughes et al. (2017): stated
intentions only. Started low because police against no police is a within-person comparison with unreported order; the
randomized strategy comparison would also end very low. Regression odds ratios not used (editors' calculation 1.36
against the printed 2.687); the paper's claim that dogs led to the most buying is unsupported (p = 0.711). Missing: the
team's 2014 pilot (a conference paper), Hickey et al. 2012 (no DOI printed), any study of behaviour.
