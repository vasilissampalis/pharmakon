## Limits

- Bluelight: robots.txt disallows "Other Drugs", its largest drug forum (opioids, stimulants, benzodiazepines, dissociatives); skipped.
- Drugs-Forum: robots.txt disallows sorting; latest threads only.
- Shroomery: permission for the robots check and the views sort went unanswered; two boards, cultivation skipped.
- Ask Erowid: three of six category pages failed. Drugs.com: first 15 per list.
- Not reached: FRANK and TripSit (no question pages), Lemmy (error), Stack Exchange (permission unanswered; archive copy not used), Quora (no topic pages).
- Views are rounded as shown; megathread parts count separately, inflating research chemicals and tapering.
