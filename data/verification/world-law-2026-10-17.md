## 2. Method

- Each participant table was read in six chunks (A-C, D-I, J-M, N-R, S-U, V-Z). Two WebFetch calls per chunk with differently worded prompts and output formats ("NAME | dates" and "name ;; dates").
- The outputs were saved verbatim in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/26a1fab4-6c74-5648-a9fc-9f33a45fba6c/scratchpad/world/readings/` and compared by `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/26a1fab4-6c74-5648-a9fc-9f33a45fba6c/scratchpad/world/scripts/compare.py`.
- A row is kept only when two readings give the same name, dates and markers.
- Where readings disagreed, the chunk was read a third time with a third wording.
- `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/26a1fab4-6c74-5648-a9fc-9f33a45fba6c/scratchpad/world/scripts/build.py` builds the list, maps names to the un.org list and runs every count and INCB check; results are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/26a1fab4-6c74-5648-a9fc-9f33a45fba6c/scratchpad/world/settled/checks.json`.

## 6. Points the caller should know

1. **VI-18 `how` comes from the printed markers, as the brief asks, and can mislead.**
   - The VI-18 column legend ends "Accession(a), Succession(d), Ratification", so an unmarked date is recorded as "ratification".
   - But for 45 states, the unmarked VI-18 date is the same date as an accession (a) or succession (d) printed in VI-15. Examples: Austria 1 Feb 1978, Azerbaijan 11 Jan 1999, Barbados 21 Jun 1976. The full list is reproducible from the settled files.
   - For these states the route was probably accession or succession, not ratification. If `how` will be shown on the site, consider not showing it for 1961.
2. **Some VI-18 dates are earlier than the Protocol's entry into force.** The VI-18 header prints entry into force as 8 August 1975, but 40 VI-18 dates are earlier (the earliest is Panama, 19 Oct 1972). The dates in the JSON are the dates printed in the tables, i.e. the dates of the instruments, not the dates they took effect.
3. **`how` values beyond the brief's four.** VI-19 prints "approval" (AA: France, Italy), "acceptance" (A: Finland, Netherlands (Kingdom of the)) and "formal confirmation" (c: European Union). These are recorded as printed.
4. **The European Union is included.** It is a VI-19 participant and is counted in VI-19's 192. It has `un_member: false` and "not party" for 1961 and 1971.
5. **Names.**
   - Names are as the UN Treaty Collection prints them. For example: "Bahamas (The)", "Czech Republic", "Naoero", "St. Kitts and Nevis", "St. Lucia", "St. Vincent and the Grenadines", "Türkiye", "Venezuela (Bolivarian Republic of)".
   - The three states in no table use their un.org names.
   - Nine treaty names were matched to a different un.org spelling: China, Côte d'Ivoire, Czech Republic, Gambia, Guinea-Bissau, the three "St." names, and Venezuela (Bolivarian Republic of).
   - Sorting ignores accents, as the UN tables do.
6. **Micronesia (Federated States of).** VI-15 prints 29 Apr 1991 (a) and VI-18 prints 29 May 1991. Both were read twice. Each is kept as printed; the JSON uses the VI-18 date.
7. **Signatory records.** The brief gives no shape for these; they use `{"status": "signatory", "date": <signature date>, "how": "signature"}`. Only Liberia (1971) has one.
8. **Schema addition.** The only added key is `status_as_at_basis` in each treaty source.
