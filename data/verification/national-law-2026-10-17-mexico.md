## 1. The official lists and their sources

Mexico's lists are in the **Ley General de Salud (LGS)**:

- **art. 234**, the list of *estupefacientes* (narcotics), unnumbered;
- **art. 245**, the *substancias psicotrópicas* (psychotropics) in five groups, fracciones I to V, each with a heading on therapeutic value.

Art. 234's closing paragraph, and art. 246 (cited as legal basis by the 1994 to 1996 listados), let the Secretaría de Salud or the Consejo de Salubridad General add substances by lists published in the Diario Oficial (DOF). The Cámara de Diputados' reform history files these under "Listado de Sustancias": DOF 24-10-1994, 26-07-1995 and 09-07-1996, and the CSG Acuerdo of 24-12-2018.

## 4. Method

- **Readings.** Each chunk was read in two WebFetch calls with differently worded prompts and output formats, `n | ENTRY` against `ENTRY ;; k`. Where they differed, a third reading was taken. For art. 234 and the Transitorios, that third reading used the second official copy.
- **Summariser limits.**
  - The summariser quotes at most 125 characters at a time and cut long chemical names at different points.
  - Long table cells made it decline once, so I asked for the columns separately.
  - Greek letters (α) are lost in the extracted text.
  - "(+)" appears where racemates should carry "±", possibly another extraction loss.
- **Comparison by script.** Python, run from the shell with the readings pasted in, so that no files other than the two outputs were written.
  - A row counts as agreed if the two readings are identical after whitespace, dash and final-stop normalisation.
  - It also counts if the readings are equal on the common prefix where one was cut.
- **Matching.**
  - One script searched every agreed entry with accent-insensitive patterns for the 77 items: Spanish INNs, code names, chemical names, plant names. The lists searched were art. 234, fracciones I to V, the four listados and the Acuerdo, and LFCPQ art. 4.
  - I checked every hit by eye and discarded these false positives:
    - BROLAMFETAMINA (DOB), for amfetamine
    - the INN TENANFETAMINA printed in the MDMA row, for MDA
    - every "-dimetilamino-" chemical name, for MDEA
    - the methadone intermediate, for methadone
    - MORFINA BROMOMETILATO, for morphine
    - PCE, PHP and TCP, for PCP
    - FURAFENTANILO, a separate entry, for furanylfentanyl
    - LEVOMETORFAN and RACEMETORFAN, for dextromethorphan (they are only context)

## 6. Disagreements between readings and how they were settled

1. **Art. 234 (2026).** Reading 2 numbered the isomers sentence as entry 136; reading 1 put it in the closing text. The substances agree: 135 rows, 114 identical and 21 equal on the common prefix. Both closing paragraphs agree. The DOF-format copy also counts 135 entries. It gives the same wording for the 19 entries checked and confirms that REMIFENTANIL, TAPENTADOL, TRAMADOL and DEXTROMETORFANO are absent.
2. **Art. 245 fracción III placeholders (2026 decree).** The readings counted 44 and 42 "..." lines before "Derogado.". They agreed on 8 between "Derogado." and ANFEBUTAMONA, on 12 between ANFEBUTAMONA and TRAMADOL, and that TRAMADOL comes last.
   - Settled by the decree's Artículo Único (paragraph 45 derogated; paragraph 56 and the last paragraph added) and the 2014 fracción III (both readings: paragraph 45 PSEUDOEFEDRINA, 55 ANFEPRAMONA).
   - The 12 placeholders match the 12 paragraphs CARISOPRODOL to TRIHEXIFENIDILO.
   - The printed placeholders do not match paragraph counts exactly: 8 are printed for paragraphs 46 to 55. The operative article governs.
3. **Fracción I table (2017), last rows.** Reading 1 had "ISOSAFROL // SAFROL" as one row (27 rows); reading 2 had two rows (28). A third reading shows each on its own line, and the 1996 listado (two readings) adds them as separate substances. Settled at 28 rows. Neither is one of the 77 items.
4. **Fracción I, chemical cell of the K2 row.** Readings 2, 3 and 5 show "NO TIENE" after "K2"; reading 4 assigns it to the next (TMA) row. Either way, no chemical name is printed for the synthetic-cannabinoid row.
5. **Fracción I, third reading.** This reading misaligned the first rows, splitting MEFEDRONA and 4-METILMETCATITONA into two rows. Readings 1 and 2 agree on one row, so the row was settled by them.
6. **FEUM compilation.** Reading 2 said GHB is in the 1985 and 1987 lists. Reading 1 shows that those lists print "Ácido gamma amino beta hidroxibutírico", which is GABOB, a separate entry from GHB in the 2026 fracción IV. Settled for reading 1; no record relies on this document.
7. **Fracción IV (2017).** Both readings print "NALOXONA NOR-PSEUDOEFEDRINA (+) CATINA" as one line and "CLORHIDRATO" as a line of its own. Kept as read; this text is superseded and was used only to identify the 2026 changes.

## 8. Other observations

- Methylphenidate and buprenorphine are *estupefacientes* (art. 234), not *psicotrópicos*.
- **2026 changes.**
  - CATINA, NORPSEUDOEFEDRINA and PSEUDOEFEDRINA moved into fracción II.
  - TRAMADOL moved from IV to III.
  - GHB and GBL entered IV.
  - All five inclusions took effect 180 days after publication. The reformed fracción IV, which no longer names tramadol or cathine, took effect on 16-01-2026. That left a gap of about six months, now past; the current status is unaffected.
- **MDMA row's INN.** The MDMA row's INN column prints TENANFETAMINA, which the site gives as MDA's INN. The MDA row prints no INN.
- **Other fracción I contents.** Fracción I also lists the PCP analogues PCE, PHP/PCPY and TCP; PIPERAZINA/TFMPP; and precursors (piperonal, isosafrol, safrol, cianuro de bencilo, APAAN).
- **Art. 234 entries without chemical names.** Art. 234 has a bare 'NITAZENO.' entry with no chemical name. It also lists both 'FURAFENTANILO.' and 'FURANILFENTANILO.', and many fentanyl analogues with no chemical names.
- **`medical_use`** records the group heading of art. 245, which states each group's therapeutic value, for example Grupo I 'valor terapéutico escaso o nulo'. Art. 234 states nothing, so it is null there.
- **FEUM compilation note.** The FEUM compilation carries an editor's note that the 1994 and 1995 listados did not follow the constitutional process to reform the law. Their substances were later written into art. 245 by the 2014 and 2017 decrees.
- **LFCPQ art. 4** (precursors) names none of the 77 items.
