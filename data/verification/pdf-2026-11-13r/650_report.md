## The review against the papers

- Irwin & Egozcue print no means (review: 23.4% and 11.0% or 11.9%); nothing says the LSD was illicit or the controls
  drug-free; Table 1 separates chromatid- and chromosome-type changes (review p. 433: not); the self-estimated average
  amount ranks with breakage (Spearman 0.86, n = 8), against the review's "no dose relation".
- Cohen, Hirschhorn, et al.: 7735 treated cells (review: 8725); no in-vitro test (review: "significantly"); LSD source
  unstated (review: "illicit"); 12.6 is a mean of six rates (pooled 11.8), including two 9-year-olds, mostly on
  prescribed medicines.
- Case 37 (52, LSD 15 times, 8 months, phenothiazines, 39 in 300) matches the Science patient (51, 15 treatments, 8
  months, chlorpromazine, 12% of 200) except age and cell count; neither paper says they are one man. If so, the review
  counts him as pure (Table 3) and illicit (Table 2). Table 5 exceeds the Science Table 1 in every condition, with
  identical peaks: possibly pooled, unstated.
