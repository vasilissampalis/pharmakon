## Known limits

- WebFetch summarizes pages and will not reproduce article text, so it is never used to read papers; it is used for
  metadata, open-access status and short official facts, each confirmed by a second fetch, a second source or a second
  reader (METHODS §12.1).
- The shell cannot reach Crossref, PubMed Central or publishers (proxy policy). PubMed Central pages and PubChem return
  bot checks to WebFetch; Europe PMC rate-limits it.
