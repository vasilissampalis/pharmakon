# Substance registry

Selection criteria (Tier 1): prevalence of use, share of drug-related harm, and coverage of every pharmacological class, so that the launch set teaches the full template. Status: **proposed, awaiting senior-author approval**; frozen at approval until launch.

Folder names are the `PDFs/<Folder>` subfolders in the project directory. Class and mechanism tags are the navigation facets; a substance can carry several.
