# General — search log (general facts about drug use, for the quiz)

Protocol: METHODS.md §2–§3 and §9; `docs/TOPIC-MAP-BRIEF-2026-10-31.md` ("Method and rules"); brief `docs/TOPIC-MAP-BRIEF-2026-10-31b.md`. Model: `DayAfter_DOI-LIST.md` and `DayAfter_search-log.md`. Run by an AI reader on 2026-10-31.

**Source.** PubMed through the E-utilities interface (`esearch.fcgi?db=pubmed`, `esummary.fcgi`; `efetch.fcgi` abstracts only where a title did not show the design), fetched with `javascript_tool` from one tab of the app's browser opened by this reader (tab on `api.crossref.org`), paced at under two requests per second. Hit counts are PubMed's `count` on 2026-10-31. Where a string returned more records than were displayed, the row says how many were screened and in which order (PubMed "relevance" or most recent first). Screening: titles and journals (abstracts where needed) against the eleven questions of the brief, preferring systematic reviews and meta-analyses, then large studies, landmark studies and their published critiques. Kept items are named by first author and year; their numbers are in `General_DOI-LIST.md`. Every listed DOI was then fetched from Crossref `works/<DOI>` in the same tab (title, journal, year, first author, volume, pages, `updated-by`, licence). Under this run's rules only PubMed E-utilities and Crossref `works/<DOI>` were used: no OpenAlex, no Crossref bibliographic search, no search engine. Where PubMed carries no DOI, a candidate DOI was fetched from Crossref `works/<DOI>` and kept only if it matched the PubMed record on title, year, first author, journal, volume and pages (noted in the row). Overlap: candidate DOIs were checked against a local index of every DOI in `search-logs/*_DOI-LIST.md` and in `data/references/*.json`.

Field tags: `[tiab]` title/abstract, `[ti]` title, `[pt]` publication type, `[MeSH Terms]`, `[au]` author.
