# Methylphenidate — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

Scope: methylphenidate (racemic dl-threo-methylphenidate and dexmethylphenidate) as prescribed for ADHD and as used without a prescription, including for cognitive enhancement. Papers comparing methylphenidate with amphetamine are shared with `Amphetamine_DOI-LIST.md` and are marked.

All DOIs below were taken verbatim from Crossref REST API responses on 2026-10-16 (search endpoint, `type:journal-article`). Every methylphenidate query also returned Crossref's `updated-by` field for each hit, so those items were checked for retractions and corrections in the same call; the 19 items that came from the amphetamine and cocaine queries (marked *(amph/coc query)*) were returned without that field. ## Known gaps this list does not yet cover (to come from the PubMed layer)

- **Long-term outcomes**: the MTA 8-year and 16-year follow-ups (Molina et al. 2009; Swanson et al. 2017 on adult height) and the ADDUCE two-year safety study (Man et al. 2023, *Lancet Psychiatry*) are not yet verified — only the MTA 24-month report (item 27). A *Lancet Psychiatry* 2023 comment on ADDUCE (`10.1016/s2215-0366(23)00092-5`) was returned but not listed because the primary article is not yet verified.
- **Cochrane 2023 update** (Storebø et al.) as a journal-indexed record, and the further published exchanges between the Cochrane authors and their critics after Banaschewski et al. 2016 (item 26).
- **Cortese et al. (2018, *Lancet Psychiatry*)** network meta-analysis of ADHD medications (shared gap with the amphetamine list).
- **Abuse liability**: Kollins's reviews comparing the abuse potential of methylphenidate with other stimulants (2001, 2003) and the human drug-discrimination and self-administration studies of methylphenidate (Rush, Stoops) were not reached.
- **Cooper et al. (2011, *NEJM*) and Habel et al. (2011, *JAMA*)** — the two large US cohort studies of ADHD medications and serious cardiovascular events — not yet verified.
- **Psychosis**: Man et al. (2016, *Transl Psychiatry*) and Hollis et al. (2019, *Lancet Psychiatry*) on methylphenidate and psychosis, which bear on item 23.
- **Childhood treatment and later substance use disorder** (Humphreys et al. 2013, *JAMA Psychiatry*; Chang et al. 2014), **growth**, **pregnancy exposure**, **overdose and poison-centre data**, and **intranasal/intravenous misuse complications** beyond Parran & Jasinski 1991.
- **Linssen et al. (2014)** review of single-dose cognitive effects in healthy volunteers and **Bagot & Kaminer (2014)** on non-ADHD youth, for the cognitive-enhancement section.
