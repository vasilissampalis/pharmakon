# MDMA — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

All DOIs below were taken verbatim from Crossref REST API responses on 2026-10-15/16 (search endpoint, `type:journal-article`), so every entry resolves to the Crossref record whose title is echoed in the table. One DOI was additionally checked with a `works/<DOI>` call to read its `updated-by` field: Ricaurte et al. 2002 (see the Retracted table). Save each PDF into `PDFs/MDMA/` with the filename given. Cross-cutting papers go in `PDFs/_Cross-cutting/`.

How this list was built: (1) section-level Crossref bibliographic searches covering the monograph sections of METHODS §8; (2) targeted title/author searches for the landmark studies of each contested question and, deliberately, for their published critiques, replies, corrections and retractions; (3) screening by title and journal (news items, conference abstracts, single case reports and predatory-looking venues dropped). The PubMed layer (strings in `MDMA_search-log.md`) is still to be run and will add to this list; its results decide what is added or dropped, not this list.

**Two retracted papers and three corrections were found. They are in their own tables below and must not be cited as evidence — only as the subject of the retraction/correction itself (METHODS §3.2).**

## Known gaps this list does not yet cover (to come from the PubMed layer)

- **Hyponatraemia**: no adequate review of MDMA-associated hyponatraemia and SIADH was reached within the Crossref query budget; the case series and the mechanism literature (vasopressin release, water loading at dance events) are missing. String P6 covers this.
- **Reneman et al. (2001, *The Lancet*)** on cortical serotonin transporter loss, the single most-cited imaging claim in this debate, is not yet verified — only the 2002 correspondence cluster and the 2002 *Synapse* validity paper. Retrieve the primary article and the four published letters replying to it (Kish; Haddad; Ratzenböck; Reneman's reply) in the PubMed layer, so the landmark is not cited without its critiques.
- **Krebs & Johansen's methodological critique** in *Neuropsychopharmacology* (to which Cowan et al., item 5, is the reply) is not yet verified; it must be obtained so the exchange is complete.
- **"Caution at psychiatry's psychedelic frontier"** (the second critique to which Mitchell et al. reply in item 18) is not yet verified.
- **The other two retracted Lykos/MAPS papers** of the three reported by the BMJ (item 25): only one (Mithoefer et al. 2019, R-3) was confirmed through Crossref in this run. Identify and document the other two, with their retraction notes (string P12).
