# Nicotine — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-15)

All DOIs below were returned verbatim by Crossref in this session (Crossref `works?query.bibliographic=` records, which carry the same metadata and the same `update`/`updated-by` fields as the `works/<DOI>` endpoint). No DOI in this list was written from memory. Save each PDF into `PDFs/Nicotine/` with the filename given; cross-cutting papers go in `PDFs/_Cross-cutting/`.

**Scope note.** This is a monograph on **nicotine**, not on tobacco smoking. The list is therefore built so that the pharmacology and toxicology of nicotine itself can be separated from the toxicology of combusted tobacco: papers that isolate nicotine (transdermal and intravenous nicotine studies, reduced-nicotine cigarettes, snus and pouches, e-cigarettes, non-nicotine smoke constituents) are prioritised over the enormous smoking-and-disease literature, which belongs to a tobacco page. Where a paper cannot make that separation, that is itself the finding and the study card should say so.

How this list was built: (1) 8 pre-specified section queries covering the monograph sections of METHODS §8 plus the substance-specific topics (nicotine pharmacology and pharmacogenetics; dependence epidemiology; e-cigarettes for cessation and the youth-uptake debate with its critiques; snus and nicotine pouches; nicotine replacement and other cessation pharmacotherapy; cardiovascular effects of nicotine itself); (2) landmark studies with the published critiques, replies and corrections attached to them — three complete exchanges are listed; (3) screening by title and journal.

**Conflict-of-interest flag.** Several papers in the harm-reduction and nicotine-pouch sections have authors with current or historical tobacco- or nicotine-industry funding. They are listed because excluding them would misrepresent the field, and they are marked `[industry-linked authorship — check COI]` so that METHODS §14 is applied when the study card is written. That marking is a prompt to check, not a finding about any individual author.

Crossref throttled this session very heavily (HTTP 429 after roughly every second request, with pauses of 90–300 s between requests as the brief requires; direct non-WebFetch access to the API is blocked in this environment). 8 of ~13 planned queries returned records. Landmarks that could not be reached are listed by title under "Known gaps". The PubMed layer (strings in `Nicotine_search-log.md`) is still to be run and will add to this list; its results decide what is added or dropped, not this list.
