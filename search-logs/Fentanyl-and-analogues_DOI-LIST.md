# Fentanyl and analogues — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-15)

Scope: pharmaceutical and illicitly manufactured fentanyl; fentanyl analogues (acetylfentanyl, carfentanil, furanylfentanyl and the rest); the benzimidazole ("nitazene") opioids as a flagged subgroup; and xylazine as an adulterant of the fentanyl supply.

Every DOI below was taken verbatim from a Crossref record returned in this session (search endpoint, `query.bibliographic`, `filter=type:journal-article`). No DOI was written from memory. Save each PDF into `PDFs/Fentanyl-and-analogues/` with the filename given; papers marked `_Cross-cutting/` go in that folder and serve several opioid monographs.

How this list was built: (1) section-level Crossref searches across the monograph sections of METHODS §8 — pharmacology and potency; acute toxicity, respiratory depression and chest-wall rigidity; overdose epidemiology; harm-reduction interventions (naloxone dosing, drug checking); market and supply; adulterants; (2) targeted author/title searches for the groups whose work this field rests on (Ciccarone and Unick on supply; Dahan and van Lemmen on respiratory depression and naloxone; Montanari and Vandeputte on nitazenes; Krieger and Bergh on fentanyl test strips); (3) screening by title and journal, dropping *Reactions Weekly* and similar trade digests, conference abstracts, single-patient anaesthesia case reports where a series exists, and analytical-method papers unless they bear on detection in the drug supply or in post-mortem interpretation. Query strings and counts are in `Fentanyl-and-analogues_search-log.md`.

Verification codes: **CS** = Crossref search record (DOI, title, container-title and year returned by the Crossref search endpoint in this session). No `works/<DOI>` re-fetches were needed. The `updated-by` field was not read, so the Retraction Watch check of METHODS §4.3 is still required at study-card stage; one erratum was nevertheless identified from the search records and is listed with its parent paper.

## Known gaps this list does not yet cover

Not surfaced by the Crossref calls that completed in this session (about half the attempted calls were refused — see the search log). Nothing below has been written from memory; each must be located and verified before use.

- **Human pharmacology and pharmacokinetics of fentanyl**: the clinical-pharmacokinetics reviews (context-sensitive half-time; transdermal, transmucosal and intravenous PK), and the lipophilicity/rapid-onset literature that underlies the potency claims. Two attempts were rate-limited.
- **Chest-wall rigidity mechanism**: Torralva & Janowsky on the noradrenergic mechanism of fentanyl-induced muscle rigidity and its relevance to overdose; Burns et al. on rigidity in overdose deaths; the anaesthesia dose–response literature. Only case reports were surfaced.
- **The naloxone-dosing debate** in full: Moss & Carlo (higher naloxone doses in the synthetic-opioid era) and the published critiques of it; Purssell et al.; Kim & Nelson; Strang on naloxone formulation and dose; Mann et al. on community naloxone dose escalation; the FDA higher-dose nasal naloxone approvals and their critiques.
- **Fentanyl test strips, effectiveness end-points**: the systematic reviews of drug-checking services and the studies that test behaviour change rather than uptake; EUDA drug-checking evidence summaries.
- **Xylazine**: D'Orazio et al. narrative review of xylazine adulteration; Friedman et al. on the national spread of xylazine in overdose deaths; Kariisa et al. (MMWR) xylazine-involved deaths; the wound-care literature and the xylazine-withdrawal management literature; medetomidine case series.
- **Carfentanil**: the carfentanil outbreak case series and toxicology (only the Ohio seizure study was surfaced); carfentanil potency estimates in humans.
- **Nitazenes**: Vandeputte's broader pharmacological characterisation series; UK nitazene death case series (Scotland, England); the naloxone-dose question specific to nitazenes.
- **Tolerance and use patterns in the fentanyl era**: the qualitative and cohort work on fentanyl tolerance, dosing frequency and withdrawal timing (two attempts rate-limited); fentanyl's effect on methadone and buprenorphine induction is covered in the Methadone-Buprenorphine map.
- **Fentanyl and stimulant co-use ("fourth wave")** beyond Ciccarone 2021; the fentanyl–benzodiazepine and fentanyl–gabapentinoid interaction literature in humans.
- **Non-US settings**: the Estonian fentanyl epidemic (Talu, Uusküla), the Baltic data, and the recent European nitazene deaths outside the UK.
