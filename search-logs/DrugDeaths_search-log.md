# DrugDeaths — search log ("How drug deaths are counted": definitions, investigation, certification, coding, and how far the counts can be trusted)

Protocol: METHODS.md §2–§3 and §9; brief `docs/TOPIC-MAP-BRIEF-2026-10-31.md` (Topic 2), with `DayAfter_DOI-LIST.md` and `DayAfter_search-log.md` as the model. Run by an AI research librarian on 2026-10-31.

**Source.** PubMed through the E-utilities interface (`esearch.fcgi?db=pubmed`, `esummary.fcgi`; `efetch.fcgi` for abstracts where a title did not show the design, setting or finding, and for the `CommentsCorrections` links that show published comments, errata and retractions), fetched with `javascript_tool` from one tab of the app's browser opened by this reader (tab on `api.crossref.org`), paced at under two requests per second. Hit counts are PubMed's `count` on 2026-10-31. Screening: titles and journals (abstracts where needed) against the six parts of the brief, in both directions (deaths wrongly attributed to a drug; drug deaths missed). Kept items are named by first author and year; their numbers are in `DrugDeaths_DOI-LIST.md`. "Kept" means kept as a candidate at that search; the final selection to the brief's target (40–60 papers) is recorded under "Final selection". Every listed DOI was then fetched from Crossref `works/<DOI>` in the same tab (title, journal, year, first author, `updated-by`), and open-access status was read from OpenAlex (`/works?filter=doi:…`, 25 DOIs per request). Existing lists were checked with a DOI index of all `search-logs/*_DOI-LIST.md` (3,534 DOIs) built locally.

Field tags: `[tiab]` title/abstract, `[ti]` title, `[pt]` publication type, `[au]` author, `[cn]` corporate author, `[MeSH Terms]`.

Totals: 27 topic searches (DF, AC, IN, PM, RE, ME) and 19 known-item searches (KI), plus one Crossref bibliographic search.
