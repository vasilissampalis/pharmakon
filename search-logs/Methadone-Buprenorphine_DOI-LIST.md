# Methadone and buprenorphine — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

Scope: the opioid agonist treatment evidence base — methadone, buprenorphine and buprenorphine/naloxone, slow-release oral morphine as a comparator, extended-release buprenorphine, and the contested questions around induction mortality, methadone QTc, buprenorphine's ceiling effect and precipitated withdrawal in the fentanyl era, diversion, treatment in prisons, and pregnancy.

Every DOI below was taken verbatim from a Crossref record returned in this session. No DOI was written from memory. Save each PDF into `PDFs/Methadone-Buprenorphine/` with the filename given; papers marked `_Cross-cutting/` serve several opioid monographs.

Verification codes: **CS** = Crossref search record returned in this session. No `works/<DOI>` re-fetches were needed. The `updated-by` field was not read, so the Retraction Watch check of METHODS §4.3 is still required at study-card stage; one corrigendum was identified from the search records and is listed with its parent paper.
