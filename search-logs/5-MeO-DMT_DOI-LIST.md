# 5-MeO-DMT — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

All DOIs below come from Crossref API responses retrieved on 2026-10-16 (never from memory). Each row's DOI, title, journal and year were returned by Crossref for that record, from bibliographic *search* responses in this session. Retraction check: none retracted; no erratum found on any retained item. Save each PDF into `PDFs/5-MeO-DMT/` with the filename given; cross-cutting papers go in `PDFs/_Cross-cutting/`.

**This is a thin literature and the list is correspondingly short — 28 papers, not 90.** The reason is substantive. The controlled human evidence consists of one phase 1 dose-ranging study of a vaporized formulation (item 2) and two small proof-of-concept clinical trials (items 3–4); everything else about effects comes from naturalistic and survey studies of ceremonial use (items 5–8, 12–13), and the safety evidence rests on a single analytically confirmed death (item 9) plus the case literature folded into cross-cutting reviews. There is no pharmacokinetic study of 5-MeO-DMT in humans with a verified DOI in this list, and no dose–response study outside item 2. The monograph should state these absences plainly rather than fill them with rodent data.

How this list was built: section-level Crossref searches (strings in `5-MeO-DMT_search-log.md`), screened by title and journal; conference abstracts, veterinary and herpetological papers unrelated to human use, and news items dropped.

## Known gaps this list does not yet cover

**Thin literature; most of what follows is missing from the field, not merely from this search.**

No verified source exists in this list for: **human pharmacokinetics of 5-MeO-DMT by any route** (the phase 1 study, item 2, reports safety and psychoactive effects; no plasma-concentration study was surfaced); **dose–response** outside item 2; **receptor pharmacology** — the 5-HT1A versus 5-HT2A question named in the brief is addressed only narratively inside item 1, because the dedicated receptor-pharmacology query returned a client error and was not re-run; **tolerance, dependence or withdrawal**; **driving**; **pregnancy**; **neuroimaging** in humans.

**Seizures and loss of consciousness**, named in the brief, are not covered by a dedicated source: the toxicity query surfaced only the 2005 death (item 9), and the emergency-presentation and case-report literature on tonic-clonic activity and unresponsiveness after inhalation — which is the single most practically important safety question for ceremonial use — remains to be found. It should be the first PubMed string run (P4 below).

Also not reached: Ermakova et al. 2022 (narrative review of 5-MeO-DMT in therapeutic contexts); Shen et al. on 5-MeO-DMT metabolism by CYP2D6; the Brazilian and Mexican ceremonial-setting fieldwork; and the GH001 phase 2b trial in treatment-resistant depression (only its intranasal counterpart, item 3, is listed).
