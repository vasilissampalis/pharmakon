# _Harm-reduction — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-15)

Cross-cutting topic: the intervention evidence every monograph's §11 draws on — drug-checking services and their accuracy; take-home naloxone and overdose education; supervised consumption facilities; needle and syringe programmes; opioid agonist treatment and mortality; safer smoking supplies; peer-delivered interventions; festival services; hepatitis C treatment as prevention; chemsex interventions. Save each PDF into `PDFs/_Cross-cutting/` with the filename given.

How this list was built: (1) Crossref bibliographic searches on the pre-registered section queries (`_Harm-reduction_search-log.md`); (2) landmark studies retrieved with their published critiques, replies and corrections, as METHODS §2.3(3) requires — the Clark 2014 / Orkin 2015 and the Marshall 2011 / Christian 2012 / authors'-reply clusters below are the two that matter most for this topic; (3) screening by title and journal (dropped: news items in *CMAJ*, *Reactions Weekly*, *PharmacoEconomics & Outcomes News* and *The Pharmaceutical Journal*; conference abstracts; trial registrations; SSRN preprints; advocacy pieces; and the large tobacco/e-cigarette and gambling "harm reduction" literatures, which are out of scope here). The PubMed and Cochrane layers (strings in the log) are still to be run and will add to this list.

**Verification.** Every DOI below was returned verbatim by a Crossref `works?query.bibliographic=` search in this session, together with the title, container-title, year and first author echoed in the table — i.e. each DOI is registered with Crossref and resolves to the paper named (the brief's verification standard for search-derived DOIs). No DOI in this list was written from memory. Items whose Crossref record carries a published correction are marked **erratum published** with the correction's own DOI; obtain both.

**Language note (METHODS §9).** Titles are reproduced as Crossref returns them. The editors' own text uses "people who use drugs" and "people who inject drugs" throughout.

## Known gaps this list does not yet cover

The Crossref search endpoint was shared with other co-editor sessions and refused roughly half of all requests with HTTP 429; seven of the eleven pre-registered queries were executed (see the log). The following are known to exist and are **not** in the list because no DOI for them was obtained by Crossref search in this session. None should enter the reference library until its DOI is verified.

- **Marshall et al. 2011 (*The Lancet*), "Reduction in overdose mortality after the opening of North America's first medically supervised safer injecting facility."** This is the single most-cited primary study on supervised consumption, and the list currently carries only its published critique (item 13) and the authors' reply (item 14). The paper itself was not returned by the supervised-consumption query, which matched on the correspondence rather than the article. **Highest-priority item for the PubMed layer**; the protocol forbids citing the critique without the original.
- **Kerr et al. 2006 (*The Lancet*), safer injection facility and syringe sharing**, and **Wood et al. 2006 (*CMAJ*), changes in public order after the opening of a supervised injecting facility** — the other two Insite primaries — not returned.
- **Drug-checking effect on behaviour.** No randomised or quasi-experimental study of drug checking changing consumption behaviour was located beyond Measham & Turnbull 2021 (item 52); the field may not contain one, which is itself a finding to state in the monographs rather than a search failure. To be confirmed in the PubMed layer.
- **Reagent testing and FTIR validation.** The analytical-accuracy literature for reagent kits and portable FTIR (as opposed to fentanyl test strips) was not reached; only Sisco 2024 (item 63) and Fabresse 2024 (item 59) touch it.
- **Naloxone RCT evidence.** The randomised comparisons of naloxone formulations and routes are in `_Overdose-and-emergency_DOI-LIST.md` (items 1–4) and are not duplicated here; the naloxone-dose-in-the-fentanyl-era question remains unsourced in both lists.
- **Hepatitis C treatment as prevention.** The modelling and trial literature on treatment as prevention (e.g. the HCV elimination modelling of Martin, Vickerman and colleagues) was only partially reached through the NSP/OST query; Vickerman 2012 (item 21) is the sole modelling paper here.
- **Heroin-assisted treatment and supervised injectable opioid treatment** (the RIOTT, NAOMI and SALOME trials and their Cochrane review) — not searched; arguably belongs to this topic and should be added.
- **Housing First, contingency management and employment interventions** as harm reduction — not searched.
- **Festival and nightlife harm reduction** beyond drug checking (medical services, welfare spaces, "crush and heat" interventions) — only Olsen 2022 and Piercey 2025 were obtained; the Wood 2010 festival paper is in `_Overdose-and-emergency_DOI-LIST.md` (item 82).
- **Overdose Good Samaritan laws.** The evaluation literature was not searched (Fletcher & Lovegrove 2019 in the overdose list is a legal history, not an outcome evaluation).
