# LSD — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-15)

All DOIs below come from Crossref API responses retrieved on 2026-10-15 (never from memory). Each row's DOI, title, journal and year were returned by Crossref for that record. DOIs marked ◆ were additionally confirmed by a `filter=doi:` record fetch that also returned the `updated-by` field (retraction/erratum check); the remaining rows come from Crossref bibliographic *search* responses in the same session. Retraction check: none retracted. One item carries a published correction (item 3) and is marked. Save each PDF into `PDFs/LSD/` with the filename given; cross-cutting papers go in `PDFs/_Cross-cutting/`.

How this list was built: (1) the Basel human-laboratory programme (Liechti group) and the Chicago low-dose programme (de Wit group), which between them carry the dose–response and pharmacokinetic sections; (2) the microdosing trials and the null/placebo findings that qualify them; (3) the population-survey literature and the 1960s alcoholism meta-analysis, each with the critiques the protocol requires; (4) section-level Crossref searches (strings in `LSD_search-log.md`), screened by title and journal — Neuroscience Applied, Eur Neuropsychopharmacol and Biol Psychiatry supplement abstracts, Reactions Weekly items and news pieces dropped.

Context note for the senior author: Crossref refused or errored on roughly half of all calls on the shared egress. Two topics (the Halpern & Pope HPPD review; LSD acute toxicity and emergency presentations) could not be covered within the window and are listed under "Known gaps".

## Known gaps this list does not yet cover

Not verified within this build and therefore left out of the tables: **Halpern & Pope 2003, "Hallucinogen persisting perception disorder: what do we know after 50 years?", Drug and Alcohol Dependence** — two differently worded Crossref queries failed (one client error, one 429) and the DOI's Elsevier PII form contains parentheses, which the `filter=doi:` endpoint rejects; obtain it by title. Also still to be found with verified DOIs: Carhart-Harris et al. 2016 (Curr Biol, LSD and ego dissolution / music); Schmid & Liechti 2018 (long-term subjective effects of LSD); Liechti 2017 (Neuropsychopharmacology review of modern LSD studies); Family et al. 2020 (LSD in older adults); Bershad et al. 2020 (microdose amygdala connectivity); Hendricks et al. 2018 (psychedelic use and criminal behaviour); Nesvåg/Bramness-type registry work on psychosis incidence.

Sections still thin: **acute toxicity, overdose and emergency presentations** — the dedicated Crossref query returned only Reactions Weekly items and unrelated overdose notes, so the monograph's acute-risk section currently rests on item 3 (safety pharmacology in a controlled setting) plus the cross-cutting adverse-event reviews (items 25–27); mass-ingestion case reports and ED series remain to be found. **LSD-related deaths**: nothing identified; the plausible route is national drug-death registries (reports R2, R8). **Driving and psychomotor impairment**: nothing identified. **Pregnancy**: no human data identified. **1P-LSD and related lysergamides**: items 49–51 are analytical and in-silico only — there is no human pharmacology for any of them, which is itself a finding for the monograph. **Dependence and withdrawal**: no dedicated source; the literature is thin by nature. **Greek data** (ΕΚΤΕΠΝ/OKANA): none.
