# _Harm-rankings — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

Cross-cutting topic: the expert-rating and multicriteria-decision-analysis (MCDA) studies that produce "league tables" of drug harm, the published critiques of that method, the toxicological margin-of-exposure alternative, and the attributable-burden estimates that are the closest thing to an outcome-based ranking. These are the papers the site must handle most carefully: every monograph will be read against a ranking, and the rankings disagree with each other and rest on expert judgement rather than measurement. Save each PDF into `PDFs/_Cross-cutting/` with the filename given.

**This is a deliberately small list — 32 papers — and that reflects the literature, not the search.** There are only four or five primary MCDA drug-harm rankings in the world, perhaps three substantial published critiques of the method, one margin-of-exposure comparative assessment with a companion, and a GBD series. The brief allows a smaller list for thin literatures; this is one. What matters here is completeness of the critique layer, not volume.

How this list was built: (1) Crossref bibliographic searches on the pre-registered section queries (`_Harm-rankings_search-log.md`); (2) each ranking retrieved together with its published critiques, as METHODS §2.3(3) requires — items 1–2 are never to be cited without items 3–4; (3) screening by title and journal (dropped: the large non-drug MCDA methods literature in operations research, engineering, finance and environmental science that these queries inevitably return; SSRN preprints; news items).

**Verification.** Every DOI below was returned verbatim by a Crossref `works?query.bibliographic=` search in this session, with the title, container-title, year and first author echoed in the table. No DOI was written from memory.

**Language note (METHODS §9).** Titles are reproduced as Crossref returns them, including "misuse" and "abuse". The site reports these rankings as *expert judgements about harm*, with the criteria, the weighting method and the panel composition always stated (METHODS §10.2), and never as measurements.

## Known gaps this list does not yet cover

The Crossref search endpoint was shared with other co-editor sessions and refused a majority of requests with HTTP 429 during this topic (five refusals for six executed searches; see the log). The following are known to exist and are **not** in the list because no DOI for them was obtained by Crossref search in this session.

- **GBD 2016 alcohol collaborators (*The Lancet* 2018), "Alcohol use and burden for 195 countries and territories"** — the companion to the above, and the source of the contested "no safe level" conclusion, together with its published critiques. Not searched.
- **van Amsterdam et al. (*Journal of Psychopharmacology* 2010), "Ranking the harm of alcohol, tobacco and illicit drugs for the individual and the population"** — the Dutch national ranking that preceded item 6. Not returned.
- **Published critiques of item 6 and item 7.** Only items 3, 4 and 5 were obtained, all of which target items 1–2. If the European and Australian rankings have attracted published critiques, they were not found; this must be settled by forward citation (Layer 4) before any of these rankings is cited, because the protocol does not allow a landmark without its critiques.
- **MCDA weight-sensitivity analyses specific to drug harms.** Item 21 demonstrates MCDA/intuition divergence in a pharmaceutical setting, not a drug-policy one. A sensitivity analysis of the Nutt weights would be the single most useful missing paper for the site's purposes, and the editors should record whether one exists.
- **The harm-ranking literature for individual novel substances** (e.g. the EMCDDA risk-assessment reports on specific NPS) is grey literature and is handled under R4.
- **Drug Harm Index work from the UK Home Office** (Macdonald et al.) and its critiques — not searched; item 12 is a recent methodological successor.
