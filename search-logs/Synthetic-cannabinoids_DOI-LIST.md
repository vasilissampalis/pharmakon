# Synthetic cannabinoids (SCRAs) — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-15)

All DOIs below were checked on 2026-10-15 against a Crossref record (`works/<DOI>` fetch, or a Crossref search record returning DOI, title, journal, year and the `updated-by` field) or, where Crossref refused (shared-IP rate limiting, see the search log), against a DOI-resolution record (publisher landing page or Semantic Scholar DOI record); the method is given per paper in the last column. No retractions or errata found among the checked records. Papers marked `R` were resolved outside Crossref and still need the Crossref/Retraction Watch retraction check (§4.3) before use. Save each PDF into `PDFs/Synthetic-cannabinoids/` with the filename given.

Scope note: the human literature on synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists is dominated by case series, poison-centre/registry surveillance and forensic post-mortem series; there are no RCTs and few cohort studies. The map therefore leans on systematic reviews of adverse events and deaths, the two NEJM outbreak/mortality papers, the landmark confirmed-consumption toxicity series (Hermanns-Clausen), and pharmacology reviews, flagged accordingly. This is appropriate to the evidence base, not an omission.

Verification codes: **CR** = Crossref `works/<DOI>` fetch; **CS** = Crossref search record (DOI, title, journal, year, `updated-by` returned); **R** = DOI resolved via publisher page or Semantic Scholar DOI record (Crossref retraction field not read).
