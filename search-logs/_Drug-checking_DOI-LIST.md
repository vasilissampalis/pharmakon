# _Drug-checking — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-26)

Cross-cutting topic, at the senior author's request (26 October): are home test kits and drug checking useful? Should people use them, do they detect what is in a drug, and does testing change what people do or prevent overdoses? The five studies already read in full (Green 2020, Laing 2021, Philp & Fu 2018, Sisco 2024a and 2024b) are not repeated here.

How this list was built: see `_Drug-checking_search-log.md`. **Verification.** Every row was checked against its Crossref record (authors, title, journal, volume, issue, pages or article number, dates, update and relation entries), fetched one at a time on 2026-10-26, and its open-access status against OpenAlex; titles are as Crossref returns them. No retraction was found; Bhai et al. 2025 has a correction (10.1177/10775587261457313, 29 June 2026) whose content could not be fetched.

**Language note (METHODS §9).** Titles are reproduced as Crossref returns them. The editors' own text uses "people who use drugs" and "people who inject drugs" throughout.
