# BeforeDuring — search log ("Before, during and after": what people do before and during use, every drug page)

Protocol: METHODS.md §2–§3 and §9; brief `docs/TOPIC-MAP-BRIEF-2026-10-31.md` (Topic 1); model files `DayAfter_DOI-LIST.md` and `DayAfter_search-log.md`. Run by an AI research reader on 2026-10-31.

**Source.** PubMed through the E-utilities interface (`esearch.fcgi?db=pubmed`, sorted by relevance; `esummary.fcgi`; `efetch.fcgi` for abstracts where a title did not show the design, and for PubMed's comment and correction links), fetched with `javascript_tool` from one tab of the app's browser opened by this reader (tab on `api.crossref.org`), paced at under three requests per second. Hit counts are PubMed's `count` on 2026-10-31; the first 12–50 records by relevance were screened for each string (number given where fewer than all were screened). Screening: titles and journals (abstracts where needed) against the four questions of the brief (preparation and state; what people do during use; supplements and medicines taken before or with a drug; what predicts a good or difficult experience). Out of scope and not kept: dose amounts, timing advice, routes compared, mixing drugs, drug checking, and anything about making a drug's effects stronger. Kept items are named by first author and year; their numbers are in `BeforeDuring_DOI-LIST.md`. Every listed DOI was then fetched from Crossref `works/<DOI>` in the same tab (title, journal, year, first author, `updated-by`), and open-access status was read from OpenAlex (`/works?filter=doi:…&select=doi,open_access,ids`, 25 DOIs per request). Overlap with existing lists was checked with a local DOI index of all `search-logs/*_DOI-LIST.md` (3,252 DOIs, including `DayAfter_DOI-LIST.md`).

Field tags: `[tiab]` title/abstract, `[ti]` title, `[pt]` publication type, `[MeSH Terms]`, `[au]` author, `[dp]` publication date.
