# _Tolerance — evidence map and PDF list, every drug page (v0.1, 2026-10-17)

Cross-cutting topic requested by the senior author: what the evidence shows about tolerance on each of the 30 drug pages — (1) tolerance in humans (controlled repeated-dose studies; people who use often compared with people who use rarely, on subjective, physiological or cognitive effects); (2) the time course after stopping (return of the response, or of receptor measures in brain imaging, toward baseline); (3) cross-tolerance between drugs in humans; (4) lower tolerance and risk (overdose after release from prison, after detoxification or residential treatment, or after other breaks); (5) reviews summarizing 1–4. Preclinical studies only where human evidence is absent, marked as such (Tier C). Grouped by drug page in the order of `data/site/substances.json`, then a cross-cutting group. Reading list only: no claims, no study cards.

How this list was built: PubMed through the E-utilities interface (`esearch`, `esummary`; `efetch` abstracts where a title was ambiguous) in the app's browser, 2–6 searches per drug plus cross-cutting searches (strings, hit counts and what was kept: `_Tolerance_search-log.md`); titles (and abstracts where needed) screened as in `docs/EVIDENCE-MAP-BRIEF.md`. A paper relevant to several pages is listed once, under its main page or under Cross-cutting, and named by number in the other pages' "Also needed" line.

**Columns.** PMID and PMC are from the PubMed `esummary` record; "—" means none. **V** = verification: `CR` = Crossref `works/<DOI>` record fetched in the browser on 2026-10-17 and matched to the PubMed record on title, year and first author, with its `updated-by` field read (retractions, corrections). Where PubMed carries no DOI, the DOI was located with a Crossref bibliographic search and then fetched the same way (noted in the search log). Nothing is listed unverified.

**Tiers.** A = what the page needs first (2–5 per drug); B = more human evidence and reviews; C = preclinical or indirect evidence (used only where human evidence is absent or as mechanism background).

Save each PDF under `PDFs/` with the path given (drug folder from `docs/TIER1.md`, or `_Cross-cutting/`).

Human cross-tolerance evidence with other depressants is old and indirect (#27–28).
