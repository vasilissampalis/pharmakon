# Prescription opioids — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

Scope: oxycodone, morphine, hydrocodone, tramadol and codeine — their pharmacology and pharmacokinetics, the evidence on long-term opioid therapy, the dose–overdose literature, the prescription-opioid epidemic and its critiques, tapering, opioid-induced hyperalgesia, benzodiazepine co-prescription, the transition from prescribed opioids to heroin, and codeine cough-syrup use ("lean"/"purple drank").

Every DOI below was taken verbatim from a Crossref record returned in this session. No DOI was written from memory. Save each PDF into `PDFs/Prescription-opioids/` with the filename given; papers marked `_Cross-cutting/` serve several opioid monographs.

**Integrity flags found during this build — read before using the papers concerned:**
- **Koren et al. 2006 (Lancet)**, the breastfed-neonate codeine/CYP2D6 case that underpins much of the codeine-in-breastfeeding guidance, now carries an **Expression of Concern** issued by the editors of The Lancet in 2026 (item 23). Both documents are listed. Under METHODS §3.2 an expression of concern is not a retraction, so the paper is kept — but it must not be used as evidence until the EoC has been read, and the claim it supports must be re-sourced.
- **Agnoli et al. 2021 (JAMA)**, the landmark tapering cohort, has a published correction: "Errors in data analysis and outcomes coding" (item 47). Obtain both.
- **Kennedy et al. 2022 (PLOS Medicine)**, the British Columbia tapering/discontinuation cohort, has a 2023 correction (item 50). Obtain both.

Verification codes: **CS** = Crossref search record returned in this session. No `works/<DOI>` re-fetches were needed. The `updated-by` field was not read, so the Retraction Watch check of METHODS §4.3 is still required at study-card stage for every item; the three flags above were identified from the search records themselves.

**Work through the list in order. Tier A first. Tell me each time you finish a batch of ten.**
