# 2C-x (2C-series phenethylamines and NBOMe derivatives) — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

All DOIs below come from Crossref API responses retrieved on 2026-10-16 (never from memory). Each row's DOI, title, journal and year were returned by Crossref for that record, from bibliographic *search* responses in this session. Retraction check: none retracted; no erratum found on any retained item. Save each PDF into `PDFs/2C-x/` with the filename given; cross-cutting papers go in `PDFs/_Cross-cutting/`.

**This is a thin literature and the list is correspondingly short — 34 papers, not 90.** The reason is substantive, not a search failure: there are only two controlled human administration studies of any 2C compound (items 1–2, both 2026, both from the Basel group and both on 2C-B), and none at all for 2C-I, 2C-E, 2C-T-7, 2C-P or any NBOMe compound. Everything else in the evidence base is case reports, poison-centre and emergency-toxicology series, forensic case work, analytical chemistry and in-vitro metabolism. The monograph's "Effects" section can therefore be written from controlled data only for 2C-B; for the rest of the series and for the NBOMe compounds it has to rest on toxicology case series, and should say so.

How this list was built: section-level Crossref searches (strings in `2C-x_search-log.md`), screened by title and journal; Reactions Weekly items, zebrafish and rodent-only work, and unindexed venues dropped.

## Known gaps this list does not yet cover

**This literature is thin, and the gaps below are mostly absences in the field rather than failures of the search.**

No controlled human administration study exists for any 2C compound other than 2C-B (items 1–2), and none for any NBOMe compound — deliberately so, given the fatalities. There is therefore no verified source for: dose–response of 2C-I, 2C-E, 2C-T-2, 2C-T-7 or 2C-P; pharmacokinetics of any 2C compound in humans (items 5, 6, 23 and 25 are in-vitro metabolism only); tolerance; dependence; driving; pregnancy. The monograph should state these absences rather than substitute animal data.

Not reached within the Crossref window and left for the PubMed layer: the Spanish human studies of 2C-B in naturalistic settings (Papaseit and colleagues' controlled-administration work; González et al. on patterns of use and subjective effects; Caudevilla-Gálligo et al. 2012 on 2C-B users) — three differently worded queries for these were refused or errored, so the only 2C-B human data in this list are the two 2026 Basel trials. Also missing: the NBOMe vasoconstriction and limb-ischaemia case reports; 2C-T-7 deaths (Curtis et al. 2003); the Dutch and Brazilian NBOH literature; 25I-NBOMe receptor pharmacology (Rickli/Liechti in-vitro profiling); and hospital presentations from the European Drug Emergencies Network (Euro-DEN).

Two items were screened out that a reader might expect: Kawahara et al. 2017 (25B-NBOMe rhabdomyolysis in zebrafish larvae, Forensic Toxicol) — preclinical, and it carries a published erratum (10.1007/s11419-017-0383-8); and Lipow 2022 ("NBOMe toxicity and fatalities: a review of the literature", *Transformative Medicine*) — the venue is not in DOAJ/MEDLINE and could not be checked, so it is excluded under METHODS §3.2 with the reason logged.
