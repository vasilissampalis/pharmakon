How this list was built: (1) landmark studies and their published critiques, replies and follow-ups; (2) systematic reviews and meta-analyses found by Crossref bibliographic search on 14 section-level queries; (3) forward snowballing of the landmark set. The PubMed layer (strings in `methamphetamine_search-log.md`) is still to be run and will add to this list; its results decide what is added or dropped, not this list.
