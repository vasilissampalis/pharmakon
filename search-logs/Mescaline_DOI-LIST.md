# Mescaline (and peyote / San Pedro) — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

All DOIs below come from Crossref API responses retrieved on 2026-10-16 (never from memory). Each row's DOI, title, journal and year were returned by Crossref for that record, from bibliographic *search* responses in this session. Retraction check: none retracted. One item carries a published corrigendum (item 15) and is marked. Save each PDF into `PDFs/Mescaline/` with the filename given; cross-cutting papers go in `PDFs/_Cross-cutting/`.

**This is a thin literature and the list is correspondingly short — 27 papers, not 90.** The modern controlled human evidence is essentially one programme: the Basel group's 2023–2025 studies of oral mescaline hydrochloride (items 1–4), which between them supply dose–response, comparative acute effects, safety pharmacology and the only human pharmacokinetics. The long-term-outcome evidence is essentially one study (item 6, Halpern 2005). Toxicity rests on a single poison-centre series (item 7). Everything else is naturalistic survey work, analytical chemistry, ethnobotany and legal-historical material. The monograph should present this as a literature with two good pillars and very little else.

How this list was built: section-level Crossref searches (strings in `Mescaline_search-log.md`), plus three mescaline papers that surfaced during the LSD, DMT and 5-MeO-DMT searches and were carried forward. Screened by title and journal; conference abstracts, rodent drug-discrimination pharmacology from the 1970s–1990s, and book reviews dropped.

## Known gaps this list does not yet cover

**Thin literature; several of the gaps below are absences in the field.**

No verified source in this list for: **neuroimaging** of mescaline in humans (none exists in the modern era); **tolerance and cross-tolerance** beyond the historical Isbell work listed under LSD; **dependence or withdrawal**; **driving**; **pregnancy**; **mescaline-related deaths** — the poison-centre series (item 7) reports no fatality, and no fatal-case series was surfaced; **interactions** with serotonergic or MAOI medication specific to mescaline.

Not reached within the Crossref window: Cassels & Sáez-Briones' "Dark classics in chemical neuroscience: mescaline" (ACS Chem Neurosci 2018) — two queries for it errored, so the standard medicinal-chemistry review is missing; Halpern & Pope's companion work on hallucinogen persisting perception disorder in the peyote cohort; Hermle et al.'s 1992–1998 mescaline PET and model-psychosis studies in Germany, which are the only pre-Basel controlled human administrations and matter for the historical record; Nichols' reviews of phenethylamine hallucinogen structure–activity relationships; and the San Pedro (*Echinopsis pachanoi*/*Trichocereus*) alkaloid-content literature, so the monograph currently has no verified source on how much mescaline a San Pedro preparation contains.

The Basel programme's forthcoming paper on emotional and cognitive empathy under mescaline exists so far only as a conference abstract (Klaiber et al., Neuroscience Applied 2026, 10.1016/j.nsa.2025.106577) and was excluded on that ground; check for the full publication before the monograph is written.
