# Cannabis — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-15)

All DOIs below were checked on 2026-10-15 against a Crossref record (`works/<DOI>` fetch or a Crossref search record returning DOI, title, journal, year and the `updated-by` field) or, where Crossref refused the request (shared-IP rate limiting, see the search log), against a DOI-resolution record (publisher landing page reached from the DOI, or the Semantic Scholar DOI record); the method is given per paper in the last column. Retraction check: none retracted among the Crossref-checked papers; two carry published errata (marked). Papers marked `R` have not had the Crossref retraction field read and must go through the Retraction Watch check at study-card stage (§4.3) before use. Save each PDF into `PDFs/Cannabis/` with the filename given; cross-cutting papers go in `PDFs/_Cross-cutting/`.

How this list was built: (1) landmark studies and their published critiques, replies, replications and follow-ups (Dunedin IQ study; EU-GEI and south-London potency studies; Mendelian randomisation; the driving meta-analyses); (2) systematic reviews and meta-analyses found by Crossref bibliographic search on 18 pre-registered section-level queries (strings in `Cannabis_search-log.md`); (3) targeted title+author queries. The PubMed layer is still to be run and will add to this list; its results decide what is added or dropped, not this list.

Verification codes: **CR** = Crossref `works/<DOI>` fetch (title and `updated-by` read); **CS** = Crossref search record (DOI, title, journal, year and `updated-by` returned by the search endpoint); **R** = DOI resolved to the intended paper via publisher page or Semantic Scholar DOI record (title, journal, year confirmed; Crossref retraction field not read).
