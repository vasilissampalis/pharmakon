# Synthetic cathinones — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

Scope: the synthetic cathinone class as a whole, with substance-level coverage of mephedrone (4-MMC), 3-MMC, MDPV, α-PVP, methylone, N-ethylpentylone (ephylone), eutylone and dipentylone. Khat and natural cathinone are out of scope.

All DOIs below were taken verbatim from Crossref REST API responses on 2026-10-15/16 (search endpoint, `type:journal-article`). From the third cathinone query onward each search also returned Crossref's `updated-by` field for every hit, so those hits are checked for retractions and corrections in the same call. Retraction check: none of the included papers is retracted; one carries a published erratum (Cameron et al. 2013, *Psychopharmacology*), marked. Save each PDF into `PDFs/Synthetic-cathinones/` with the filename given.

The human literature is thin: there are controlled-administration studies only for mephedrone, methylone and (since 2024) 3-MMC; for MDPV, α-PVP, N-ethylpentylone and eutylone the human evidence is case series, poison-centre data and post-mortem toxicology, and the pharmacology is preclinical. This is said again under "Known gaps".

## Known gaps this list does not yet cover (to come from the PubMed layer)

- **Thin human literature (stated as such for the monograph).** Controlled human administration exists only for mephedrone (items 11, 16), methylone (14, 15) and 3-MMC (12, 13). For MDPV, α-PVP, N-ethylpentylone, eutylone and dipentylone the human evidence is case series, poison-centre data and post-mortem toxicology; their pharmacology and abuse liability come from animal and in-vitro work (items 4, 31–39). Eutylone in particular has no human study and only one behavioural animal study in this list (item 39).
- **Mephedrone human pharmacokinetics and intranasal administration** beyond Papaseit et al. 2016 (the Olesti/Papaseit pharmacokinetic papers and the intranasal self-administration study) were not reached within the query budget.
- **Mechanism landmarks not yet verified:** Baumann et al. (2012, *Neuropsychopharmacology*) showing mephedrone and methylone are transporter substrates; Simmler et al. (2013, *Br J Pharmacol*) in-vitro characterisation of designer cathinones; Eshleman et al. (2013).
- **α-PVP-specific clinical series** (the "flakka" presentations in Florida; Swedish STRIDA MDPV and pyrovalerone series by Beck et al.) and **MDPV hyperthermia/multi-organ failure series** (Borek & Holstege; Froberg).
- **Mephedrone neurotoxicity** — the Angoa-Pérez/Kuhn papers reporting that mephedrone does not damage dopamine nerve endings in mice, and their contrast with MDMA and methamphetamine.
- **Injecting cathinones outside chemsex** (Hungary, Romania, Ireland, Scotland): HIV and hepatitis C outbreaks linked to injecting of cathinones (e.g. the Romanian and Hungarian outbreaks), and the Glasgow HIV outbreak.
- **Dependence and withdrawal** (only one survey item, 62, addresses dependence), **psychosis and agitation series**, and **UK mephedrone-related deaths after the 2010 ban** (Loi et al.).
- **Drug-checking data**: cathinones sold as MDMA and as other drugs, and EUDA drug-checking network findings.
