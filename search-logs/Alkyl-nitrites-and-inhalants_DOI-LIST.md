# Alkyl nitrites and inhalants — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

All DOIs below come from Crossref API responses retrieved on 2026-10-16 (never from memory). Each row's DOI, title, container-title, year and first author were returned by Crossref for that record. Save each PDF into `PDFs/Alkyl-nitrites-and-inhalants/` with the filename given; cross-cutting papers go in `PDFs/_Cross-cutting/`.

This folder covers two literatures that barely cite each other and should be kept visibly separate in the monograph: **alkyl nitrites ("poppers")** — amyl, isobutyl and isopropyl nitrite, nitric-oxide donors used mainly as sexual-context vasodilators, whose characteristic harms are retinal, haematological and haemodynamic; and **volatile solvents and gases** — toluene, butane, propane, difluoroethane, aerosols and glue, whose characteristic harms are leukoencephalopathy, sudden death and adolescent-onset dependence. Items 1–13 and 23–44 are the nitrite literature; items 14–22 and 45–62 the solvent and gas literature.

How this list was built: (1) 11 section-level and targeted Crossref bibliographic queries covering nitric-oxide-donor pharmacology, maculopathy, methemoglobinemia, PDE5 interaction, chemsex context, legal-status change, solvent neurotoxicity, sudden sniffing death, adolescent epidemiology, deaths and treatment; (2) the landmark studies with their published critiques (Davies 2012 with Fajgenbaum's photic-injury alternative; Filley 1990 and Rosenberg 1988 with Ranson's rodent-model failure to replicate); (3) a topic-level Crossref `has-update:true` retraction and erratum screen. All query strings are in `Alkyl-nitrites-and-inhalants_search-log.md`. The PubMed layer (strings in the search log) is still to be run and will add to this list; its results decide what is added or dropped, not this list.

**Work through the list in order. Tier A first.**

## Known gaps

**Sudden sniffing death and volatile-substance mortality is the largest hole in this list.** Four separate Crossref queries aimed at it (on "sudden sniffing death", on butane and propane fatalities, on UK volatile-substance-abuse mortality, and on inhalant-related deaths generally) were refused — three with target errors and one with a rate-limit refusal — or returned the general cardiology literature instead. Bass's 1970 JAMA description of sudden sniffing death, the Shepherd and Anderson forensic series, and the St George's annual mortality series (report R1) are all missing from the tables and must be added from the PubMed layer. Item 9 covers deaths from nitrites only, and only in the UK.

**Treatment of inhalant use disorder.** No controlled trial and no systematic review of treatment were surfaced. The Cochrane search in Layer 3 and PubMed string P11 are the next step; the Canadian Paediatric Society's statement on interventions for inhalant use among First Nations youth appeared in one hit list and should be retrieved as grey literature alongside report R10.

**Papers that exist and should be in the tables but were not surfaced in the rate-limited window:** Williams & Storck 2007 (Pediatrics, the AAP clinical report that supersedes item 59); Balster's reviews of the behavioural pharmacology of inhalants and Bowen's abuse-liability work; Garland & Howard on inhalant use disorder course; Crossin's later reviews of acute and chronic inhalant effects; Romanelli et al. 2004 (Pharmacotherapy) on amyl nitrite use and harms; Giorgetti et al. on chemsex pharmacology; the ophthalmic follow-up literature after Rewbury 2017; and any human study of the alkyl-nitrite/PDE5-inhibitor interaction itself — item 34 gives the mechanism in healthy men taking sildenafil with a nitric-oxide donor, but no study of poppers plus a PDE5 inhibitor in people who use both was found, which is itself worth stating in the monograph.

**Evidence that appears not to exist:** no controlled human study of the subjective or cognitive effects of alkyl nitrites; no cohort study of people who use poppers; no neuroimaging in poppers users (the eye is the only organ with imaging evidence); no pregnancy data for either half of this folder; no drug-checking data on the current isopropyl-nitrite products beyond item 5's contents analysis. Greek data: none.
