# Amphetamine — evidence map and PDF list (v0.1, 2026-10-16)

Scope: amphetamine as a substance — prescribed amphetamine salts and lisdexamfetamine, and non-medical and illicit amphetamine ("speed"). Methamphetamine has its own list (`methamphetamine_DOI-LIST.md`) and is not repeated here; papers that pool "amphetamine-type stimulants" appear in both and are marked.

## Known gaps this list does not yet cover (to come from the PubMed layer)

- **Cortese et al. (2018, *Lancet Psychiatry*) network meta-analysis** of ADHD medications in children and adults — the single most-used efficacy source for this section — did not surface in the Crossref queries run and is not yet verified. Likewise the MTA long-term follow-ups and Swanson's growth-suppression analyses.
- **Habel et al. (2011, *JAMA*)** on serious cardiovascular events in young and middle-aged adults taking ADHD medications, and **Westover & Halm's** systematic review of stimulants and cardiovascular events: the beta of this section rests on Zhang 2024 and Liu 2018 alone until these are added.
- **Ricaurte et al. (2005, *JPET*)** on dopaminergic nerve-ending damage in non-human primates at amphetamine doses said to match ADHD treatment — the primary study at the centre of the therapeutic-dose neurotoxicity debate (items 8–10 are the review and the critique). It must be obtained with the replies it drew, and read in the light of the same group's retracted MDMA paper (see `MDMA_DOI-LIST.md`).
- Amphetamine **withdrawal** syndrome specifically (as distinct from methamphetamine withdrawal), **injecting-related harms** in European amphetamine-injecting populations, and **amphetamine and pregnancy**.
- **Dependence liability of prescribed versus illicit amphetamine**, and the question of whether treatment with stimulants in childhood raises or lowers later substance use disorder (the Humphreys/Molina meta-analyses).
- **Enantiomer pharmacology in humans** beyond Smith & Davis (1977): the d:l ratio of mixed amphetamine salts and its clinical consequences.
- European **amphetamine purity and adulterant** data (caffeine, creatine, paracetamol) from drug-checking services.
